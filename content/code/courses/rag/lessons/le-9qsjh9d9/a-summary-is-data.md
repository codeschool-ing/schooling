---
title: A summary is data
version: 1
---

A summary of a customer's conversation is a new record about that customer, written by a model. Three
consequences, each of which a team meets later if it does not decide now.

**It is personal data, derived.** Lesson 13 deleted Rafael's turns in one statement because every row
carried his account. A summary of those turns carries the same facts and must carry the same account,
be listed when he asks what is held about him, and go when he asks to be forgotten. A summary stored
in a field nobody remembered is the copy that survives the erasure.

**It is the model's words, not the customer's or the company's.** A summary that says "the customer
agreed to store credit" may be a model's misreading of "I would prefer a refund rather than waiting".
Kept beside the original turns, it is a convenience that can be checked; kept instead of them, it
becomes the record, and its mistakes become facts. This is why lesson 13 stored the turns as written:
compaction decides what goes into the prompt, and the table keeps what was said.

**It carries forward whatever it summarised.** A summary of a conversation that contained an
instruction aimed at the assistant, pasted in by a customer or copied from a document, can preserve
that instruction in a shorter, more prominent form, now in a part of the prompt the program wrote
itself. Lesson 16 is about keeping text from outside away from the instructions; a summary is text
from outside, however much it has been condensed, and is placed and labelled as such.
