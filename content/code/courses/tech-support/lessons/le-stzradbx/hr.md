---
title: HR: people joining, moving and leaving
version: 1
---

HR and support meet at three moments in every employee's time at the organisation, and each has a
checklist on the support side:

- **Joining.** Account, e-mail, computer, access to the systems the role needs, ready on the first
  morning. Support needs the date, the role and the manager, in advance, through the agreed channel:
  a request that arrives the same morning is a new employee sitting at an empty desk.
- **Moving.** A person who changes department gets the new access **and loses the old**. Keeping both
  is how people end up, years later, with access to everything they ever touched.
- **Leaving.** Accounts disabled at the agreed time, not a week later; equipment returned and checked
  against the register, lesson 11; shared passwords they knew changed.

Two things about how. **Act on HR's request, not on the hallway**: a technician who hears that someone is
being dismissed does nothing until HR asks, and says nothing to anyone. And **the timing of a departure
can be sensitive**: when access must end during a meeting, support is told the time, and it happens at
that time, not before.
