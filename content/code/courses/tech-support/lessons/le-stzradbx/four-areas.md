| area | what they need from support | what support needs from them | how to say it |
|---|---|---|---|
| management | a decision to make, with options and their cost | the decision, and priorities, lesson 6 | the impact first, the options, a recommendation |
| HR | access ready when people join, gone when they leave | notice, in advance, through the agreed channel | dates, names, systems |
| legal | data handled as the law says, evidence untouched | clear requests, in writing | facts, times, who did what |
| vendors | evidence that the fault is theirs to fix | repair within the contract | serials, error messages, steps already taken |

Two rules run through all four. **Write it down**: a hallway conversation with any of them is not a
request and not an agreement, and the ticket records what was asked and decided. And **use their words,
not yours**: *the shared folder filled up and a report could not be saved* works for everyone in this
table; *the ext4 volume hit 100%* works for nobody in it.
