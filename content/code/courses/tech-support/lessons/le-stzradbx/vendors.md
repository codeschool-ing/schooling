---
title: Vendors: evidence first
version: 1
---

A vendor repairs what the contract says, when it can see that the fault is theirs. The request that gets
a repair quickly is the one that answers the vendor's questions before they ask:

```localised
Subject: Printer OFFICE-2, stops with "paper jam, tray 2" without a jam

Serial:        the printer's serial, from its label or its status page
Contract:      the support contract's number
Fault:         the queue stops with "paper jam, tray 2" several times a day;
               there is no paper in the path when we check
Since:         the date of the first ticket about it
Already done:  tray 2 removed and checked; the rollers cleaned; the fault continues
               with tray 2 empty and printing from tray 1
Evidence:      the print queue's history, attached
Impact:        the only printer on the second floor
Our ticket:    the service desk's ticket number, for your reference
```

Three habits save days. **Keep both ticket numbers**, theirs and yours, in both tickets. **Record every
contact**: who you spoke to, when, what was promised. And **know the contract's terms**, lesson 6's SLA
from the other side: a vendor that promised a technician on site in one business day and has not come is
escalated to their account manager, not waited for.
