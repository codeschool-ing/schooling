- **Registered** means it has a tag, a physical label with the organisation's number, and a line with
  both halves filled in.
- **Assigned** records who has it and where. When it moves desk or person, the line changes the same
  day; a register updated once a year is right for one day a year.
- **In repair** says it is out of the building, with whom, since when, and under which warranty.
- **Retired** comes before **disposed**, and the step between them is the one with legal weight: **the
  data on its disk is wiped**, and the wipe is recorded. A computer sold or given away with a former
  employee's files on it is a data leak under the LGPD, lesson 13.

Once a year, or when an area moves, **count**: walk the floor, read the tags, and compare with the
register. What is on the floor and not in the register, and what is in the register and not on the floor,
are both findings, and the second is the one that costs money.
