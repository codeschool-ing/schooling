---
title: What an inventory answers
version: 1
---

An **inventory**, or asset register, is the list of everything the organisation owns that support looks
after: computers, monitors, printers, phones, and the software licences on them. It answers questions
nobody can answer otherwise:

- **What do we have, and where?** The day a floor moves, or an auditor asks.
- **Who has it?** When somebody leaves, the register says what they have to hand back.
- **Is it in warranty?** Before paying for a repair the vendor would have done for free.
- **What should be replaced next?** By age, by model, by the number of tickets about it.

It also joins to the rest of this course: a ticket names a computer, lesson 5, and the register says what
that computer is, whose it is and how old it is, without asking the user. **Two halves** fill a register:
what the machine can say about itself, and what only people know. This lesson collects the first half
with a script and names the second.
