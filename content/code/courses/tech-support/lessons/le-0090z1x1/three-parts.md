1. **What happened**, in the words of their work: *the shared folder was full*, not *the file system
   reached 100%*.
2. **What it means for them.** This is the part most often skipped, and the one they were waiting for:
   *nothing new could be saved there, so this morning's report was not saved*. If something of theirs
   was lost, say so here, plainly.
3. **What happens next**: what you did, what they should do now, and whether anything is still pending,
   with a time. *It has space again: save the report now. I have asked the team that runs the export
   to stop it filling up again, and I will write to you tomorrow.*

Three sentences are often enough. **The order matters**: a person who hears the cause first is still
waiting to know whether their work is safe, and hears nothing else until they do.
