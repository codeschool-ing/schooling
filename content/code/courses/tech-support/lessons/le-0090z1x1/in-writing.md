title: In writing
version: 1
---

A written reply, in the ticket or by e-mail, follows the same three parts, with a few rules of its own
because nobody can ask you what you meant:

- **The first sentence is the answer.** *Your report can be saved again.* The explanation comes after,
  for whoever wants it.
- **Steps are numbered**, one action each, with the exact name of what to click as it appears on their
  screen, in their language.
- **No internal words.** The ticket's category, the queue's name, "L2", "P3": those are the support
  team's words, lessons 6 and 7, and mean nothing to the person reading.
- **Say when the ticket will close**, and what they do if the problem comes back: reply to this
  message, not open a new one, so the history stays in one place, lesson 5.

A written reply is also a record. Everything in it may be forwarded to their manager, and should read
well there too.
