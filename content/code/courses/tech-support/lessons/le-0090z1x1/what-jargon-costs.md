---
title: What jargon costs
version: 1
---

Jargon is not the technical words themselves. It is **a technical word used with somebody who has no
use for it**. "The DNS resolution failed" is exact, and to Elisa, in accounts, it means nothing she can
act on. It costs twice:

- **She cannot act.** An explanation exists to let a person decide something: wait, try again, save
  their work elsewhere, call a client back. A sentence she cannot understand leaves her where she was.
- **She feels small.** Nobody likes to be spoken to in a language they do not have. Some people ask
  again; many nod, and the next time something breaks, they do not open a ticket and try to fix it
  themselves.

The fix is not to avoid every technical word, which leads to explanations so vague they are wrong. It is
to **say what happened in terms of what the person does**, and to keep the technical detail for the
ticket, where the next technician needs it, lesson 5.
