---
title: Without belittling anyone
version: 1
---

Most condescension is not in what a technician says, it is in **small words** that slip in:

| the word | what it tells the person | instead |
|---|---|---|
| *just*, *simply* | this was easy, so you should have managed | drop it: "click Save" |
| *obviously*, *of course* | everybody knows this except you | drop it |
| *as I said* | you were not listening | say it again, differently |
| *you should have* | the fault is yours | "next time, …" |
| *don't worry about it* | you would not understand | explain the one part that matters to them |

Two opposite mistakes sit on either side of good service:

- **Over-explaining**: a lecture on how DNS works to somebody who asked when they can print. Answer the
  question they asked, and offer more: *"I can show you what happened, if it's useful."*
- **Talking down**: explaining as if to a child, or with a tone of patience that sounds like effort. A
  person who does not know computers knows their own job well; talk to them as the expert in that.

And **check understanding by asking what they will do**, not *"does that make sense?"*, to which the
polite answer is always yes. *"So if the printer stops again, what will you look for?"* finds out in one
answer.
