The three follow the same pattern, and each exam's page on comptia.org has the current details:

- **Question types.** Multiple-choice questions, and **performance-based questions**, which ask you to do
  something in a simulation: configure a setting, put steps in order, match items. The second kind is why
  labs matter: it asks what you would type or click, not what you remember reading.
- **Where.** At a test centre run by Pearson VUE, CompTIA's exam provider, or online with a remote proctor
  watching through your camera. The online option has its own rules about the room and the computer, and
  they are strict.
- **Time, number of questions, passing score, price and languages** are all on the exam's page, and all
  of them change between versions. Check them there, not in a course or a video, including this one.

**Renewal.** CompTIA's certifications are valid for three years, and are renewed through its continuing
education programme: by earning credits from training and work, or by passing a higher certification.
The terms for each one are on the same site.
