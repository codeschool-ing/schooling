title: What was wrong with it
version: 1
---

The article is short and reads clearly. Everything wrong with it is something it leaves out:

- **A value that changes, written in as a constant.** The address was true once. An article that copies
  a changing fact will go stale; one that says how to look it up stays right.
- **No way to check.** It ends with *open it in the browser*, and says nothing about what should appear.
  A reader who gets an error has no way to tell whether they followed it wrong or it is wrong.
- **A title nobody searches for.** "Intranet on a new computer" describes the task; a person with the
  problem searches for what they see, *intranet does not open*, or the error message.
- **A date with no owner.** *Last reviewed* says when someone looked, not who is responsible for it
  being right, and two years is long enough for a server to move.
