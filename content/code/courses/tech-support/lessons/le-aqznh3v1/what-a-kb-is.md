title: What a knowledge base holds
version: 1
---

A **knowledge base**, KB, is the team's written memory: articles that explain how to do something or
what to do about a known problem. It sits between the other two documents of this course:

| document | what it records | read by |
|---|---|---|
| a ticket, lesson 5 | one problem, one time | whoever works that problem again |
| a runbook, lesson 8 | a procedure for a recurring problem, step by step | whoever is on duty when it recurs |
| a KB article | how to do something, or what a known error means | anyone who searches for it, sometimes users themselves |

Its value is entirely in being used, and it is used when two things are true: **someone can find the
article** when they have the problem, and **the article is still right** when they follow it. An old
article fails the second, and the next section shows what that costs.
