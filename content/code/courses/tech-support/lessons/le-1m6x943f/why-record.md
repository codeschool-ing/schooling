---
title: Who reads a ticket
version: 1
---

A ticket looks like a conversation between a user and a technician. It is read, later, by people who
were not in it:

- **The next technician**, when the same fault comes back, or when this one is handed on, lesson 7.
  What they need is what was tried and what it showed, including what did not work.
- **The user**, who may read it again to remember what they were told, and whose manager may read it
  too.
- **Whoever looks for patterns.** Ten tickets saying *the shared folder is full* are one problem with
  a cause, and nobody sees that unless each ticket says it in words that can be found.
- **Whoever measures the service**, lesson 6: when it was opened, when somebody started, when it was
  solved.

**A ticket that says "fixed" serves none of them.** It closes the conversation and keeps nothing.
