---
title: When what you see is a problem
version: 1
---

Sometimes what a technician sees is not private but wrong: signs that an account is being used by
someone else, company data on a personal storage service, material that may be illegal. Keeping quiet is
not the answer then, and neither is investigating.

1. **Stop** where you are. Do not open more files to find out how far it goes.
2. **Do not copy, move or delete anything**, and do not confront the person. Either could destroy what
   lesson 12 called evidence, or warn someone who should not be warned.
3. **Write down what you saw**, when, on which computer, and how you came across it, doing the ticket.
4. **Report it through the channel the organisation names**, security, your manager or legal, and to no
   one else.

A technician is not an investigator, and the step that feels most helpful, *let me just check a bit
more*, is the one that turns a clean report into an intrusion. What you saw while doing your job is a
report. What you went looking for afterwards is a technician reading someone else's files.
