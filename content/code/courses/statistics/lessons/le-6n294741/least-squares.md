A **regression line** summarises how one variable, the **response**, changes with another, the **predictor**. For Horta, the response is delivery minutes and the predictor is distance.

The line has the form

**predicted minutes = a + b × km**

where *a* is the **intercept** and *b* is the **slope**. The question is which line to draw, since any line through the cloud could be called a summary.

## Residuals

For each delivery, the **residual** is the observed value minus the value the line predicts. The first delivery in Horta's data was 2.0 km and took 23.5 minutes. The line, found below, predicts 30.00 minutes for 2.0 km, so the residual is 23.5 − 30.00 = **−6.50**: it was six and a half minutes quicker than the line says.

## Least squares

The **least-squares line** is the one that makes the **sum of the squared residuals** as small as possible. Squaring does what it did for the standard deviation in lesson 5: it stops positive and negative residuals from cancelling, and it penalises a big miss more than several small ones.

For any data there is exactly one such line, and its slope and intercept have a formula:

**b = r × (s of y ÷ s of x)** and **a = mean of y − b × mean of x**

The slope is the correlation, rescaled from standard deviations into the units of the data.

## Horta's twelve orders

For items and baskets, lesson 17 found r = 0.996, with standard deviations of 4.20 items and R$ 59.42. So b = 0.996 × 59.42 ÷ 4.20 = **14.09 reais per item**, and a = 79.17 − 14.09 × 6.25, which comes to **−8.88** when the unrounded values are used. The line is

**predicted basket = −8.88 + 14.09 × items**

An order of 10 items is predicted at −8.88 + 140.88 = **R$ 132.00**.

## The 120 deliveries

With distance in A2:A121 and minutes in D2:D121:

```localised
=SLOPE(D2:D121, A2:A121)        2.48227842382101
=INTERCEPT(D2:D121, A2:A121)    25.0326047440283
```

**predicted minutes = 25.03 + 2.48 × km.** The residuals of a least-squares line always add up to zero: the line passes through the middle of the cloud, with as much above it as below.
