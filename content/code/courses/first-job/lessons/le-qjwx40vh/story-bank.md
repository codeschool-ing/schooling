---
title: A bank of stories
version: 1
---

The questions vary, but they draw on a small number of themes, and **five or six real stories cover almost
all of them**. Write them before the first interview, as STAR notes:

1. Something you are proud of. The portfolio project is the obvious one.
2. **A problem you solved** that somebody else could not, or had not.
3. **A mistake you made**, what you did about it and what changed after. (The last section.)
4. **A disagreement** with a colleague, a teacher or a customer, and how it ended.
5. **Something you learned fast** because you had to.
6. **A time you helped somebody**, a colleague, a classmate or a user.

**The stories do not have to come from IT.** A first job's candidate has worked in shops, restaurants,
call centres, family businesses; those are real stories about customers, pressure and mistakes, and
interviewers for junior roles expect them.

Then check that each story can answer several questions. The Saturday at the phone shop is a problem
solved, a difficult customer and a disagreement with a manager, depending on which part you stress.
