title: Who you already know
version: 1
---

"I know nobody in IT" is almost never true, because the list is longer than the people you would call
friends. Write it down, in this order:

1. **People you studied with**, on any course, including this one, who now work somewhere.
2. **Teachers and instructors**, who know many former students and often hear of openings first.
3. **People from previous jobs**, in any field. The manager at the shop may have a cousin who runs an IT
   company.
4. **Family and neighbours**, asked plainly: *does anybody at your company work in IT?*
5. **People at the companies on your list** whom you have never met, which is the last group and the
   hardest, and the next section's message is written for it.

**In Brazil**, informal networks (the friend of a cousin, the neighbour's company) matter a great deal in
hiring, and nobody finds it strange to be asked. The same is true in most places; it is only more visible
here.

Put the names in the tracker from lesson 8, with a column for what you asked and when. A referral is an
application, with a date and a next step.
