---
title: Keeping the pipeline alive
version: 1
---

The mistake that costs the most time in a first job search is **stopping everything for one process**. You
reach the final interview at the company you like best, stop applying while you wait, and when the no
arrives three weeks later, the top of the funnel is empty and has to be filled from nothing.

So, until an offer is signed:

- **Keep applying at your usual pace**, however promising one process looks. Lesson 8's routine takes ten
  minutes an application.
- **Keep the tracker current**, including the processes that ended, with what you learned.
- Tell a process that is further ahead when another one speeds up. *I have a final interview elsewhere
  next week; is there a date for your decision?* It is normal, and it is the honest way to make two
  processes arrive at the same time.

And look after the person doing all this. A search can take months; rejections accumulate in a way
applications do not. **A fixed number of hours a day**, and days with none, keeps it a project rather than
the whole of life.
