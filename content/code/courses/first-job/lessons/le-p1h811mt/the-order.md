title: The order on the page
version: 1
---

With the page cut down, the order decides what the ten seconds see. For a first job it is:

1. **Name, city, contact and links**, on two lines.
2. **One line** saying what you do, in the advert's words.
3. **The strongest evidence**: the portfolio project, or a job where you did this work. Two or three lines
   each, written the way lesson 4 shows.
4. **Experience**, most recent first, including jobs outside IT. A year in a shop taught you to deal with a
   customer who is upset, which is half of support.
5. **Education**: the course, the institution and the year, or "in progress".
6. **Skills**, five or six lines, chosen from the advert and each one something you could be asked about.

**Adapt the top for each application.** The line under the name and the choice of skills change with the
advert; the rest stays. Lesson 8 shows how to do that in ten minutes rather than an hour.

**Write it in the language of the advert.** An advert in English gets a CV in English. Do not add a
language to the skills section that you would not use in the interview: the interviewer may switch to it.
