---
title: The last five minutes
version: 1
---

The end of an interview decides what happens in the following week, and three things belong in it:

1. The next step and the date. *What happens next, and when should I expect to hear?* This is the date
   lesson 10's follow-up waits for.
2. A correction, if you need one. If you answered something badly and realised later in the interview,
   this is the moment: *earlier I said X about backups; I should have said Y.* It reads as care, not
   weakness.
3. **One sentence on why you want it**, specific to what you heard today: *hearing about the two weeks
   shadowing the queue makes this the kind of start I was looking for.*

Then thank them by name, and send the short thank-you from lesson 10 the same day or the next.

What does not belong: asking how you did, which puts the interviewer in an awkward position and gets an
answer that means nothing either way.
