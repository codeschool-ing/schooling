---
title: Questions whose answers reveal something
version: 1
---

A good question to an interviewer has an answer that **you could not have found anywhere else, and that
tells you what the job is really like**. Four that work for almost any junior role:

1. **What would I be doing in my first month?** The answer shows whether they have thought about it. A
   specific answer (*shadowing the N1 queue for two weeks, then taking tickets with a buddy*) is a team that
   onboards people; *you'll pick it up as you go* is a team that does not.
2. **Who would I go to when I'm stuck?** Lesson 2's question, asked of the person who knows. A name and a
   habit (*your lead, and we pair twice a week*) is what a first job needs.
3. **How do you know somebody in this role is doing well after six months?** It tells you what they will
   judge you on, and whether they know.
4. **What is the hardest part of this job?** People answer this honestly more often than you would expect,
   and the answer is the thing the advert left out.

Ask the technical interviewer about the work and the team, and the recruiter about the process. **Do not
ask what you could read on the website**, and do not start with holidays and home-office days; those come
at the offer.
