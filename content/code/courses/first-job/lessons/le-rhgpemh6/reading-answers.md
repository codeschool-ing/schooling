title: Reading the answers
version: 1
---

The answers are information, and some of it is the kind nobody says outright. What to listen for:

- **Vagueness about the first month** usually means nobody is responsible for your first month.
- **"You'll be very independent here"**, on a junior role, is lesson 2's warning: independence is
  something you grow into, not something you start with.
- **The hardest part is people, not technology** (*the users are demanding*, *priorities change weekly*).
  That is not necessarily bad; it is the thing to ask one more question about.
- **High turnover mentioned in passing** (*the last two people in this role…*). Ask what happened.
- The interviewer cannot say how success is measured. Then you will be measured on impressions, which
  is harder for somebody new.

None of these decides anything on its own, and a good team can give a clumsy answer. Write them down with
the rest in the tracker; **when two offers arrive at once (lesson 18), these notes are what you compare**.
