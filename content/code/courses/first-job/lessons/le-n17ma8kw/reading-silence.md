---
title: Reading a silence
version: 1
---

A silence after an application almost never means *we read your CV and decided you were not good enough*.
The common causes are duller:

- **The role was filled** by somebody further along, often an internal candidate or a referral.
- **The role was paused**: a budget moved, a manager left, a project was cancelled.
- **Nobody has read the applications yet**, because the person who should is doing three other jobs.
- **The process does not send rejections**, which is common and unkind, and not about you.

So after the follow-up and about three weeks, **mark the application cold in the tracker** and move your
attention to the next ones. Cold is not closed: roles reopen, and a recruiter who goes quiet in June writes
in September. Keep the row; stop checking the inbox for it.

The one silence worth reading is a pattern: **many applications, all silent**, which is lesson 8's leaking
top of the funnel, and the fix is in the CV and the aim, not in more follow-ups.
