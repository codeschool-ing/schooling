---
title: The follow-up message
version: 1
---

A follow-up is shorter than the first message, and it adds one thing:

> Hi Mariana, a quick follow-up on my application for the junior IT support role (ref. 2031), sent on the
> 3rd. I'm still very interested, and since then I've added a recorded demo of the lab (link). Happy to talk
> whenever suits you. Ana

What makes it work:

- **It says which role and when**, because the reader may be handling thirty.
- **It adds something new**, if there is anything: a demo, a finished retrospective, a certification exam
  passed. If there is nothing new, say you are still interested and stop there; do not invent news.
- It asks for nothing difficult. No *could you tell me the status of the process?*, which the recruiter
  may not know or may not be allowed to say.

What does not work: guilt (*I haven't heard back*), pressure (*I have other offers*, when you do not), and
reminding them of their own advert's deadline.
