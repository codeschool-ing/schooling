---
title: What the call checks
version: 1
---

A screening call (*triagem*, or *entrevista com o RH* in many Brazilian processes) lasts fifteen to thirty
minutes, and the person on the other end is usually not technical. They are checking whether it is worth
putting you in front of the people who are, and they check **four things**:

1. That you are who the CV says. They will ask you to *tell me about yourself*, and then about one or
   two lines of the CV. Two minutes, told the way lesson 19 of the portfolio course told a project:
   what you do, the evidence, what you are looking for.
2. That you communicate clearly. Not fluently or confidently; clearly. Short answers that answer the
   question, and saying *I don't know* when you do not.
3. That the practical facts fit. Salary, start date, contract, location, hours. The next section.
4. That you know what the role is. *Why did you apply?* is asking whether you read the advert.

None of these is technical, and **none of them can be improvised well**. The answers to 1 and 3 are
written in advance; 2 is practised by saying 1 aloud to somebody; 4 is reading the advert again the morning
of the call.
