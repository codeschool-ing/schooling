---
title: The practical questions
version: 1
---

The questions that end most screening calls badly are the practical ones, because they are asked quickly and
answered on the spot. Write your answers before the first call and keep them next to the phone:

- Salary expectations. Lesson 17 is about this; for the screening call, have **a researched range** and
  a sentence to say it with. *Based on what I've seen for junior support roles in São Paulo, between X and
  Y, depending on the benefits.*
- Contract. In Brazil, the recruiter may ask whether you accept *CLT* or *PJ*. Know which you would
  accept and at what difference in pay; lesson 17 does the arithmetic.
- Start date. *Immediately*, or the notice you owe a current employer. Under a *CLT* contract that is
  usually the *aviso prévio*; say how many days.
- Location and hours. On-site, hybrid or remote; shifts; weekends. For support roles, **ask about the
  shift pattern** if the advert does not say, because it is the thing most likely to make an offer
  impossible to accept.
- Availability for the next steps. When you could do a technical interview, and whether you can take an
  afternoon off for it.

Honesty is cheaper here than anywhere else in the process. A salary or a shift you cannot accept, hidden
now, is discovered at the offer, after everybody has spent weeks.
