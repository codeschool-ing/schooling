---
title: Your questions at the end
version: 1
---

Every screening call ends with *do you have any questions?*, and *no* is a missed opportunity. The recruiter
knows the process better than anybody, so ask about **the process**, not about the technology:

1. **What are the next steps, and roughly when?** Now you know when to follow up (lesson 10), and whether
   there is a take-home exercise (lesson 13) or a live interview (lesson 12).
2. **Who would I be working with day to day?** Lesson 2's question, asked at the first chance.

One more is worth asking if the advert did not say: **the salary range**. In Brazil it is increasingly
normal to ask, and a recruiter who has a range usually shares it; asking early saves everybody's time.

Then thank them, and **write down what they said** the same day: names, the steps, the dates, the range.
It goes in the tracker. By the third process running at once, you will not remember which company said
what.
