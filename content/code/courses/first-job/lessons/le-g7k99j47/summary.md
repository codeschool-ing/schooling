---
title: The summary and the rest of the profile
version: 1
---

The *About* section is the one place on the profile with room for a paragraph, and it is read by somebody
who has already clicked, so it can do more than the headline. Keep it to **four or five short lines**:

1. What you do, in one sentence.
2. The evidence: the portfolio project, with its result and a link.
3. What kind of role you are looking for, and where (city, remote, hybrid).
4. How to reach you.

The rest of the profile mirrors the CV, with two differences. **Experience** can hold more detail than the
one-page CV, because nobody reads it in ten seconds; still, each entry should start with what you did.
**Featured** (a row of links near the top) is the best place on LinkedIn for the project: the repository,
the deployed address or the recorded demo.

**A photo belongs here**, unlike on the CV: a LinkedIn profile without one reads as abandoned. A clear,
recent picture of your face is enough; nothing about it needs to be professional.
