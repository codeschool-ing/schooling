**In Brazil**, benefits are a large part of a CLT offer and vary more between companies than salaries do.
The common ones, and what to check in each:

- **Health plan** (*plano de saúde*): whether it covers dependants, and whether there is *coparticipação*,
  a share you pay each time you use it.
- **Meal and food allowances** (*vale-refeição*, *vale-alimentação*): the monthly amount.
- **Transport** (*vale-transporte*): required by law for employees who commute, and the law lets the
  employer deduct up to 6% of your base salary for it. For a remote role, ask what replaces it.
- **Others**: a remote-work allowance, training budget, gym, profit sharing (*PLR*). Ask how PLR was paid
  in the last year rather than what the maximum is.

Put a monthly value on each, as best you can, and add it to lesson 17's comparison. **Two offers with the
same salary can differ by a large share of it in benefits.**

**Equity** (*stock options*, sometimes in start-ups) is a right to buy shares later at a fixed price.
Three words describe it: **vesting**, the schedule on which the right becomes yours; the **cliff**, a first
period (often a year) before anything vests; and the **strike price**. For a first job, **value it at zero**
when comparing offers. It may be worth something one day; you cannot pay rent with it now, and most junior
offers do not include it.
