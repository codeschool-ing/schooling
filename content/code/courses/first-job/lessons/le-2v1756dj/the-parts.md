title: The parts of an offer
version: 1
---

When an offer comes, the right first answer is **thanks, and a request to see it in writing**: *that's
great news, thank you. Could you send me the details so I can read them properly?* No company worth
joining withdraws an offer because you asked to read it.

Read each part, and write down what is missing:

- Contract. *CLT* or *PJ*; the start date; whether there is a probation period and how long (lesson 21).
- Pay. The monthly gross, and **when it is reviewed**: a promise of a raise after probation is worth
  having in writing.
- Benefits. Lesson 17 compared CLT and PJ on pay alone; the next section is about what the benefits add.
- Work. The job title, the hours, **the shift pattern** for support roles, and whether it is on-site,
  hybrid or remote, with how many days.
- Small print. The clauses in the third section.
- Equity. Stock options or shares, if any; rare in junior offers, and explained at the end.

An offer that exists only as a phone call or a chat message is not yet an offer. **Ask for the details in
writing** before you resign from anything or turn down another process.
