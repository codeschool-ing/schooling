title: The funnel
version: 1
---

Every job search is a funnel: many applications at the top, fewer answers, fewer calls, fewer interviews,
and, eventually, an offer. Each stage is **a fraction of the one above it**, and a first search usually
has small fractions, because the CV has no job on it yet.

That drawing comes from an invented tracker, written for this lesson, and its numbers describe that file
and nothing else. The shape is the point, and it has two consequences.

**The top has to be wide.** If one application in six gets any answer, a week with three applications is a
week with no news, and three weeks of that feel like rejection when they are arithmetic.

**The stage that leaks tells you what to fix.** Many applications and no answers: the CV or the aim.
Answers but no calls after the screening: how you come across on the phone, or the salary conversation.
Interviews but no offers: the technical part, or the questions at the end. Each of those has a lesson in
this course, and **you only know which one you need if you wrote the stages down**.
