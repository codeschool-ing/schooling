Volume without aim is the two hundred identical applications from the video, and aim without volume is
three perfect ones a month. The way between them is **a routine that aims one application in about ten
minutes**:

1. **Read the advert for the three lists** of lesson 1: the work, the day-one needs, the nice-to-haves.
   Two minutes.
2. **Change the line under your name** so it uses the advert's words for the work. One minute.
3. **Choose the five or six skills** that match the day-one needs, from a longer list you keep. Two
   minutes.
4. **Check the top of the CV** answers the two questions of lesson 3 for this advert. If the project is
   the evidence, is it the first thing under the line? Two minutes.
5. **Save it with a name that says which one it is**, export the PDF, apply, and **add the row to the
   tracker**. Three minutes.

Keep a master CV with everything, and make each application's version from it. The master changes when you
learn something new; the versions are disposable.

How many a week depends on the market in your city and your field, and there is no honest number to give.
**Enough that the tracker has something to say after a month** is the only rule that holds everywhere.
