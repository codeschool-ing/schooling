---
title: Scope and time
version: 1
---

A take-home arrives with a brief and, usually, a suggested time: *about three hours*, *this should take an
evening*. **Take the suggested time seriously**, for two reasons. The reviewer will compare your work with
what is reasonable in that time, not with what you could do in a week; and a submission that obviously took
four evenings tells them you either did not read the brief or cannot estimate.

Read the brief the way lesson 4 of the portfolio course read one: **what it must do, what it must not do,
and what counts as done**. Then cut to the smallest version that meets it, which is lesson 5 of that course
in a single afternoon.

If there is no suggested time, ask: *roughly how long do you expect this to take?* It is a normal question,
and the answer tells you how deep to go.

**When the time runs out, stop**, even if something is missing, and say what is missing in the notes. A
submission that is honest about what it did not do beats one that silently took twice the time, because
the reviewer is also checking how you will behave with a deadline at work.
