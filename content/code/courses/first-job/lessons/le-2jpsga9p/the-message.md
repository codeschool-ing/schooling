---
title: The message to a recruiter
version: 1
---

In Brazil the letter's more useful cousin is **a short message to the recruiter** who posted the job, on
LinkedIn or by e-mail. It is read on a phone, between other messages, so it has to work in **four or five
lines**:

> Olá, Mariana. I applied today for the junior IT support role (ref. 2031). I built and documented a
> small-office lab, with a restore that was tested — the runbook is here: [link]. I'd be glad to talk
> whenever suits you. Ana

That is the whole thing: **who you are, which role, the one piece of evidence with a link, and an easy next
step.** No CV attached (they have it), no paragraph about motivation, and no question they have to research
to answer.

Send it **after applying**, not instead of applying: a recruiter can do little with a message about a role
you have not applied for, except ask you to apply. And send it once. Lesson 10 deals with what to do when
there is no answer.
