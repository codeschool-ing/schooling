---
title: Three paragraphs
version: 1
---

A letter worth reading fits on a phone screen. Three paragraphs, each doing one thing:

**Why this role.** One or two sentences that could not be sent to any other company. *Your advert asks for
someone to maintain accounts and document procedures for twelve schools; I built and documented a
small-office environment in a lab, and the runbook is linked below.*

**The evidence.** The one thing in your CV most relevant to this advert, told the way lesson 4 tells a
project: what you did, what it achieves, how to check. If there is something to explain (the career change,
the gap), it goes here, in one plain sentence and without apology.

**What happens next.** When you are available and how to reach you. *I can start in two weeks and am
available for a call any afternoon.*

What to leave out: the list of adjectives, the story of how you fell in love with computers, and every
sentence that begins *I believe I would be a great fit*. Say why instead.

Write it for the person who will read it. If the advert names a hiring manager, address them; otherwise a
plain greeting (*Hello*, *Olá*) is fine.
