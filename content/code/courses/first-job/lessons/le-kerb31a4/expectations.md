---
title: Expectations, in writing
version: 1
---

The single most useful thing to do in your first week is to **ask your manager what good looks like at the
end of the trial period**, and write the answer down:

> *What would you like to see from me by the end of the first three months? If it helps, I'll write it down
> and we can look at it together halfway.*

Most managers have an answer in their head and have never said it. Asking gets it said, and writing it down
turns a guess into a list: *close tickets in the N1 queue without escalating the common ones; know the
backup routine; document one procedure that is missing.*

Then **check it halfway**, at around day 45: *here's the list we made; this is where I think I am; is that
how it looks from your side?* If there is a gap, you hear about it with six weeks left to close it, not on
the last day.

If your manager will not or cannot say what good looks like, write your own version from lesson 16's
questions and the work you see around you, and show it to them. **A list they correct is better than no
list.**
