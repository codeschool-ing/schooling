Two habits, and everything in this lesson is one of them.

**Know which machine you are talking about.** The modem, the router and the radio share a box and
fail differently, and half the questions here are answered by naming which one the symptom
belongs to.

**Place the problem before touching anything.** A second device, a second site, the settings page
and the line light are four questions and two minutes. Every expensive mistake in a home network
— the reset that cleared the settings, the faster plan that changed nothing, the repeater in the
worst room — is what happens when they are skipped.
