Four things, and the last lesson of this course is the shortest to summarise.

**A password is long, and it is used once.** Length beats punctuation, and reuse is what actually
costs people their accounts.

**A second factor turns a stolen password into nothing.** On the e-mail address first, because
everything else resets through it, and write the recovery codes down.

**Updates are automatic, including the restart.**

**And a scam is a shape rather than a story**: urgency, authority, a channel you did not choose,
and a request for a code, a password, a payment or your machine. Leave the conversation and call
back on a number you found yourself.
