Two questions run under all of this.

**Which part does this symptom belong to?** Heat is slow-when-warm. A disk is slow-on-one-file
and makes a noise. Memory wanders between programs. Power wanders between everything and follows
load. Anything that changes when you move it is mechanical.

**And does it mean stop?** Four things do: a swollen battery, a burning smell, a clicking disk,
and liquid. Everything else in this lesson can wait until after the files are somewhere else.
