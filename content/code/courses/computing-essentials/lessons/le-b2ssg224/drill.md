One idea, three times: the shape of a socket is the only thing you can see and the least
informative thing about it. The protocol, the speed and the power are three separate negotiations
that the connector does not report.

One question gives you the back of a machine with the names taken off. The rest are about the
same habit: **when something does not work, name the four links and change one.**
