One triangle: range, speed and power, and nothing gets all three. Each of the four technologies
here picked a corner, and everything else about it is the consequence.

The second idea is the one that answers most real problems: the air is shared, so a slow evening
is usually a queue rather than a weak radio. When a question describes a symptom, ask which corner
the device is in and whether anybody else is in the room.
