**A regime change is a lasting change in how a series behaves**: a new price, a competitor opening
next door, a product withdrawn, a pandemic, a change in how the number is counted. The series after
the change is not the old series plus noise. It is a different series, and the history before the
change describes it less well every week.

Panela raised its prices on 1 September 2025, and orders stepped down. Lesson 4 showed what that
does to a forecast made before it. This section shows the quieter harm: what it does to the tools
that *describe* the past.

**STL read part of the price rise as a change of season.** Its October-to-December index slid from
1.023 in 2023 to 0.947 in 2024 and 0.880 in 2025: the method was allowed to let the season drift, and
a drop that happened to start in September looked to it like the autumn becoming quieter. Nothing in
the data says it was wrong; one autumn of a new regime is indistinguishable from a season that is
changing. Only somebody who knows about the price rise can tell it apart, which is the general
lesson: **a model cannot know about an event nobody told it about.**

Once it is told, it can measure it. The step regressor estimates the price rise at −15.3%, a
proportional fall in orders after September, with the trend and season around it held in place.
`panela.py` wrote the rise as a factor of 0.86 on the level, a fall of 14 per cent, so the estimate
is close to the truth this one time it can be checked. That number is useful far beyond forecasting: it is the first estimate of what the price decision
cost in volume, which is what somebody will ask.

## What to do after a regime change

- **Mark it.** Put the date and the cause in a table that every forecast reads. The next analyst
  will see the step and wonder.
- **Model it, when the step is clean**: a step regressor as above, or an intervention in the trend
  such as Prophet's changepoints. The forecast then continues from the new level.
- **Or train only after it**, when the old regime no longer tells you anything about the new one.
  The cost is less history, and a seasonal model needs at least one full year of the new regime to
  learn its season.
- **Never let a smoother quietly absorb it.** A trend or season that bends to fit a step will be
  wrong on both sides of it.
