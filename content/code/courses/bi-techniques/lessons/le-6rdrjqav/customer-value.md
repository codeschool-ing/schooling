Retention says how many customers stay; **customer value says how much money a customer brings in as
they stay**, and it is the number a marketing budget needs. The standard measure is cumulative revenue
per customer acquired, by age: everything a cohort has spent so far, divided by everybody who joined
it, including those who have left.

In the first month the three channels are almost identical, about 380 reais per customer: everybody
places a first order of the same few box sizes. By the end of the first year **a referral customer
has brought in 4,245 reais, a search customer 3,756 and a social customer 2,646**. The difference is not
what they spend per order; it is how long they keep ordering, which is the retention curve of two
sections back multiplied by the price of a box.

## What value is for

The question the number answers is **how much it is worth paying to acquire a customer from each
channel**. If acquiring a customer through social media costs as much as through search, social is
a worse channel by about a third of a year's revenue per customer, and the gap is still widening at
month 12.

Three corrections turn this revenue into a number that can set a budget.

- **Revenue is not profit.** Ingredients, packing and delivery come out of every box; the value that
  pays for acquisition is the margin, a fraction of the revenue that finance knows and this data
  does not hold.
- **A real twelve months beats an extrapolated lifetime.** The common formula, *average monthly value
  ÷ monthly churn rate*, assumes customers leave at a constant rate forever. Real curves usually
  flatten, because loyal customers leave more slowly; Panela's data, built with a constant chance of
  leaving, is the rare case where that assumption holds. Where twelve or twenty-four real months exist, report them and
  say what is assumed beyond.
- **Money later is worth less than money now.** For horizons of several years, future revenue is
  discounted at the company's cost of capital; over one year the correction is small.

Customer value also inherits every caution of lessons 12 and this one: it is computed per cohort, it
needs cohorts old enough to have reached the age reported, and the 2025 cohorts, which retain better,
will be worth more than these 2024 figures say.
