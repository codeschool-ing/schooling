title: A method before the tools
version: 1

"The internet is down" can mean a loose cable, a wrong setting on one laptop, a DNS server that stopped,
a provider's outage, or one website that is broken for everybody. Guessing costs time, and the tools
answer different questions, so the order they are used in matters.

**Check from the bottom up, one step at a time, and stop at the first one that fails.** A step only
means something if the one below it passed. A failed `ping` to a name says nothing until a `ping` to an
address has worked, and a web page that does not load says nothing about DNS until `dig` has answered.
Each ticket in this lesson stops on a different step, and the step is the diagnosis.

Two habits make the method work. Test with **addresses** as well as names, because a name involves DNS
and an address does not. And write down what each step printed, because the person who takes over the
ticket, or the provider on the phone, will ask.
