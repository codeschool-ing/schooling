title: Who else is on the team
version: 1
---

Lessons 12 to 18 followed tickets through developers' hands. Most teams have other people in them, who
write no code and shape almost everything the code does. At the bakery there are three:

- **Marta** looks after the **product**. In Scrum she is the product owner from lesson 15.
- **Paulo** is the **designer**. He decides how the site looks and, more importantly, how it works for a
  customer holding a phone in one hand and a coffee in the other.
- **Diego** does **QA**, *quality assurance*. He has appeared since lesson 16 as the person who tests.

## What each one does, and what they need from you

| | their question | what they need from a developer |
|---|---|---|
| product | *why* are we building this, and what comes first? | an honest cost for each option, early enough to choose |
| design | *how* will a person use it? | questions about the cases the drawing does not show |
| QA | *how* can it break? | what changed, and how to try it |

**Product** decides what is most important and why, and is responsible for whether the work was worth doing.
Marta does not decide how it is built, and a developer does not decide what matters most; each needs the
other's information to decide well.

**Design** is more than colours. Paulo talks to customers, draws the screens, and decides what the page says
when something goes wrong. His drawings usually show the **happy path**, the case where everything works,
because that is the first thing anybody draws. The other cases are where a developer's questions help most.

**QA** is not the last gate before release, and it is not a person who clicks around at the end. Diego thinks
about how things fail: a negative balance, a slow connection, a card entered twice. He can do that much
better **before** the code exists, in refinement, than after it has been built on the wrong assumption.

## What you need from them

The same things, in the other direction. From Marta, the reason behind a ticket and a clear answer about
what matters most. From Paulo, the design before building, and the states he has not drawn yet. From Diego,
the cases he would try, before you have written the code he will try them on.
