The certificates of the ITIL framework from lesson 8 are the standard credential for IT operations, service desks and anybody whose job touches incident, problem or change management. Like PRINCE2, they are issued by PeopleCert.

## The scheme

- **ITIL 4 Foundation** is the entry point. It tests the vocabulary of lesson 8 — the service value system, the guiding principles, the main practices — and has **no prerequisite**. It is by a wide margin the most-held ITIL certificate, and many IT job adverts in Brazil list it.
- Beyond it, ITIL 4 has **practitioner and specialist** modules on particular areas, from creating and supporting services to strategy, and two designations that combine several modules: **Managing Professional** and **Strategic Leader**. The module names have been reorganised more than once since 2019, which is exactly the kind of change this lesson's first section warned about; check the current list before planning a path.

## Renewal

ITIL 4 certificates, like the other PeopleCert families, must be **renewed within three years**: by passing another exam from the ITIL 4 family, which renews all of them, or by retaking the same exam.

## Who it is for

ITIL Foundation is worth having for anybody who works with an operations organisation, which includes most architects in large companies. Knowing what a normal change is, why a problem is not an incident and what a service level agreement commits to is the vocabulary of lesson 8, and the certificate is a cheap way of showing you have it. The higher modules make sense for people whose career is in service management itself.
