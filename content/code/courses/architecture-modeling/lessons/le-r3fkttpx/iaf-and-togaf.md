**IAF and TOGAF are not rivals so much as answers to different questions: TOGAF is strongest on the
process of doing enterprise architecture, IAF on the structure of the content it produces.** Capgemini
contributed to TOGAF's development, and the two have influenced each other; using both together is
common in organisations that know both.

| | TOGAF | IAF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **centre of gravity** | a process: the ADM cycle | a structure: the grid of levels and areas |
| **who maintains it** | The Open Group, an open standard | Capgemini |
| **the parts of the enterprise** | four domains: business, data, application, technology | four aspect areas: business, information, information systems, technology infrastructure |
| **from reasons to products** | baseline, target, gap; architecture and solution building blocks | contextual, conceptual, logical, physical |
| **notation** | ArchiMate, a sister standard | none of its own; ArchiMate or any other |
| **certification market** | large | small |

## How they map

The useful mapping is between IAF's levels and TOGAF's phases:

- IAF's **contextual** level is TOGAF's Preliminary phase and Phase A: principles, drivers, scope, the
  vision;
- IAF's **conceptual** and **logical** levels are the content of phases B to D: what the target needs,
  and the architecture building blocks that provide it;
- IAF's **physical** level is phase E's solution building blocks, the products chosen.

So a team can **run TOGAF's cycle and organise each phase's content with IAF's grid**. When Tainá did
Carcará's phase C, she used the conceptual and logical levels as the headings of the information
systems section, which kept the product choices out of it until phase E.

## Which one an employer means

A job advertisement that says *TOGAF or similar* usually means *you can work at the enterprise level,
with a method*. Knowing one framework well and the shape of the others is what that asks for. In a
company that grew with Capgemini's help, IAF's vocabulary, *contextual* and *conceptual* in
particular, will be in the documents you inherit, and recognising it is half of reading them.
