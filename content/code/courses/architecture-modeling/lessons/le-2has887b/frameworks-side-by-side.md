**The frameworks of lessons 1 to 10 are easier to keep apart when they are lined up by what kind of
thing each one is, what it is for, and at what scale it works.** This section is the comparison the
last three lessons have been building towards.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Zachman** | ontology | enterprise | six questions for six audiences; every description has a cell | 10 |
| **TOGAF** | method, with content framework | enterprise | the ADM cycle from vision to governed change | 8 |
| **IAF** | taxonomy and approach | enterprise and solution | why, what, how, with what, in order | 9 |
| **ArchiMate** | notation | enterprise | one typed language across business, application and technology | 9 |
| **FEAF, DoDAF, NAF** | reference models and viewpoints | government and defence | descriptions many parties can combine | 9 |

## Three questions that sort them

When a new framework comes along, three questions place it quickly:

1. **Is it a method, a taxonomy, a notation or a template?** Lesson 9's distinction. Two of the same
   kind compete; two of different kinds usually combine.
2. **What scale is it for?** An enterprise framework applied to one service produces paperwork; a
   system framework applied to an enterprise misses the business layer.
3. **What is its one idea?** Every framework that lasted has one. If you cannot say it in a sentence,
   you have not understood the framework yet, and probably neither has the person recommending it.

## They agree more than they differ

Lined up, the frameworks keep saying the same few things in their own words:

- **separate the audiences**: Zachman's rows, 42010's stakeholders, C4's levels, 4+1's views;
- **separate why from what from how from with what**: Zachman's rows again, IAF's levels, TOGAF's
  building blocks, lesson 6's *what, not how*;
- **trace each decision to its reason**: IAF's ordering, TOGAF's requirements management, lesson 5's
  records and lesson 6's matrix.

**An architect who has those three habits can work inside any of these frameworks within a week.**
One who knows the frameworks' vocabulary without the habits will produce the documents and miss the
point, which is the outcome lessons 8 and 9 warned against.
