**Corporate job descriptions for architects ask for frameworks by name, and it is worth knowing what
they mean by it.** The names on the list and what an interview actually tests are not the same thing.

## What appears on job descriptions

In the corporate market, especially banks, insurers, telecoms, energy and government, the names that
come up for architect roles are, most often, **TOGAF**, with **ArchiMate** beside it, a mention of
**Zachman** or **frameworks such as Zachman**, and, for business-facing roles, **BABOK**. **C4**
and **ADRs** appear more and more in product companies and in engineering-led roles. IAF appears
where Capgemini's influence is strong. Defence and public-sector work names DoDAF, NAF or the
government's own framework.

Certifications appear too, usually as *desirable* rather than *required*: TOGAF Foundation and
Practitioner, ArchiMate, and the IIBA's certifications for business analysts.

## What is actually tested

An interview for these roles rarely tests the framework's content directly. Questions of the kind
interviewers ask look like these:

- *Walk me through how you would approach a change that affects five systems and two departments.*
  This is TOGAF's ADM and lesson 7's stakeholders, asked as a situation.
- *How do you document an architecture so that people still use the documents a year later?* This is
  lessons 5 and 11.
- *Draw the architecture of a system you worked on.* This is lessons 1 to 4, and the interviewer is
  watching whether you name the question, the audience and the notation before you draw.
- *How would you present this to the board?* This is lesson 14.

**The framework names get the CV read; the habits get the job.** A candidate who can say *I used a
tailored ADM, kept baseline and target in ArchiMate, and recorded decisions as ADRs next to the code*
has said, in one sentence, that they know three frameworks and know how to choose among them.

## For a first architect role

If you are aiming at your first architect position and have time to study one framework deeply,
the names on the job descriptions point to **TOGAF**, with **ArchiMate** beside it.
If you are in a product company, **C4 and decision records** done well will serve you better than any
certificate. And whatever you choose, keep the three habits from the previous section; they are what
transfers.
