**BABOK's Business Analysis Core Concept Model, the BACCM, names six concepts that are present in
every change, and says that business analysis is about understanding all six and how they relate.**
It is the most useful page of the guide for an architect, because it is a checklist small enough to
hold in your head.

| concept | what it is | Joana's reconciliation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **change** | the act of transformation in response to a need | moving from manual monthly matching to an automatic daily one |
| **need** | a problem or opportunity to be addressed | Finance needs two days a month to close, and mismatches reach the auditors |
| **solution** | a specific way of satisfying needs in a context | a reconciliation job comparing the gateway's settlement files with Carcará's payments, with a screen for the exceptions |
| **stakeholder** | a group or individual with a relationship to the change, the need or the solution | Joana, the finance director, Payments, Sales, the external auditors, the payment company |
| **value** | the worth, importance or usefulness of something to a stakeholder | two days a month back for Finance; fewer audit findings; earlier detection of a gateway error |
| **context** | the circumstances that influence and are influenced by the change | the gateway's file format; Brazilian tax rules; cash refunds at station counters, which the gateway never sees; São João volumes |

## The concepts are connected

The model's real use is the relationships. Each concept is defined in terms of the others: a
solution satisfies a need, in a context, delivering value to stakeholders, through a change. So a
gap in one shows up as a question about another:

- *What need does this solution meet?* If nobody can say, the solution came first. **Automate the
  reconciliation** was a solution; the need was found by asking Joana what the two days are for.
- *Value to whom?* The finance director values closing the month faster. Payments values not being
  paged at night by a job that fails on a malformed file. Both are value; they pull different ways.
- *What in the context could change the solution?* The gateway announced a new settlement file
  format for next year. A solution built on today's format has a date on it.

## Using it on Monday

Before a requirements workshop, write the six headings on a page and fill in what you know. The
empty ones are the agenda. When Tainá did it for the reconciliation, **context** was the emptiest:
nobody in the room knew that station agents refund cash at the counter for tickets bought by card,
so the money leaves through the back office and the gateway never sees it. That one fact changed the solution, and it came from asking a question the model
made obvious.
