---
title: Status, supersession and a log that stays readable
version: 1
---

**A record's status says whether it still applies, and the rule that keeps a log trustworthy is that
an accepted record is never rewritten.** When a decision changes, a new record is written that
supersedes the old one, and both stay in the log. The history of *why* is then as readable as the
current answer.

- **Proposed**: written, open for comment, usually as a pull request.
- **Accepted**: agreed and in force. From here, the text is frozen apart from typos and links.
- **Rejected**: discussed and not adopted. Keeping rejected records is worth it: they are the
  options somebody will propose again.
- **Deprecated**: no longer applies, and nothing replaces it, for example because the component it
  was about was removed.
- **Superseded by ADR-N**: replaced by a newer decision, which says *Supersedes ADR-M* in turn.

ADR-0004 decided, in 2023, to hold seats as rows in the booking database. It was the right decision
then: one store, no new infrastructure, a few hundred holds a minute. ADR-0009, in 2025, superseded
it, because the forces had changed: peak traffic had grown five times and the database was the
bottleneck. **Both records are true**, each about its own time. If ADR-0004 had been edited to say
*Redis*, a reader would have no way to know that the database approach had been the original one,
that it had worked for two years, or why it stopped working. That is what somebody needs to know before proposing to move holds back to the database.

## Keeping the log navigable

- **Number in sequence, never reuse a number.** A record's number is its stable identity; the title
  may be improved, the number never changes.
- **Link both ways.** The new record says what it supersedes, and the old record's status says what
  superseded it. That status change is the one edit an accepted record receives.
- **An index.** A single page listing every record with its number, title, status and date. Tools
  can generate it; a hand-written table works too.
- **Date the status.** *Accepted 2025-05-12* tells a reader how old the forces are.
