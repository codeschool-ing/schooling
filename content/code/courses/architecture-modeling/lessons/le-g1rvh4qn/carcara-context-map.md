## Reading it

- **Fleet → Sales, with an anticorruption layer.** Frota is upstream: it was bought in 2009, its
  model of a *viagem programada* has forty fields and codes nobody at Carcará remembers, and it will
  not change. Sales translates it into its own *Trip* at the boundary. Before the map, that
  translation was spread across eleven classes; the map named the layer, and the team moved the
  translation into one place.
- **Pricing → Sales, customer and supplier.** Pricing is upstream, but Sales's needs count in its
  plans: when Sales needs a fare quote for a group booking, Pricing schedules it.
- **Sales → Boarding, open host service with a published language.** Sales publishes the day's
  ticket list in one documented format, and Boarding is **conformist** to it. That format is also
  what the drivers' phones store offline, which is why changing it needs a migration plan for 300
  phones.
- **Payment gateway → Payments, with an ACL.** The gateway is another company's open host service;
  Carcará's Payments context translates its statuses into Carcará's own.
- **Sales and Notifications, open host and conformist.** Notifications takes the *ticket sold*,
  *ticket cancelled* and *trip changed* events as Sales publishes them.
- **Finance, separate ways.** Finance reads the gateway's settlement files and does not integrate
  with Sales at all. That is a decision, and drawn as one it can be questioned: it is also why the
  monthly reconciliation takes Joana two days.

## What the map changed

Three decisions came out of one drawing, which is typical:

1. The ACL between Fleet and Sales became a component with its own tests, and Frota's next upgrade
   broke one class instead of eleven.
2. The published language to Boarding got a version number, so a change to it can be rolled out
   while old phones still run.
3. The *separate ways* line to Finance was reopened, and lesson 7 follows the requirements work
   that came out of it.

**Draw it yourself.** Add a Customer Service context to the map, used by the agents who answer the
phone. Which contexts is it downstream of? Is it conformist, or does it need its own model of a
ticket? Write the pattern at each end of every line you add.
