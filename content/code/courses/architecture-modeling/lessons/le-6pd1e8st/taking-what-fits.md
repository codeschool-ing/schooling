**TOGAF is often criticised as heavy, and the criticism is fair of TOGAF applied whole. Applied as
its own standard asks, tailored, it is a set of good questions with names.** The useful skill is
knowing which parts pay for themselves at which size.

## The criticism, stated fairly

- **Too many deliverables.** The content framework lists dozens of artefacts. A team that produces
  them all spends its time documenting rather than deciding.
- **Too slow for how software is built.** A phase-by-phase cycle sits awkwardly beside teams that
  ship every day.
- **Too abstract.** It says what to produce and much less about how to make a good architecture
  decision, which is the hard part.

Each criticism is true of a certain way of using the framework, and the 10th edition's series guides
on agile and digital practice exist because The Open Group heard them.

## What Carcará kept

| kept | why |
| --- | --- |
| **the four domains** | they made the business layer visible: the cash refunds and the cash report |
| **Phase A's statement of work** | two pages that got the board to agree on scope and success before money was spent |
| **baseline, target and gap analysis** | it found the capabilities the target forgot |
| **transition architectures** | 26 stations could not switch on one day, and the in-between states were the hardest |
| **principles with implications** | they let teams predict the board's answers |
| **dispensations with end dates** | exceptions stayed exceptions |

## What Carcará skipped

The full content metamodel, most of the catalogue of artefacts, a formal repository tool, and the
finer grades of compliance. **Nothing was lost that anybody missed**, and the decision to skip them
is itself written down in the Preliminary phase's tailoring, so the next architect can revisit it.

## The rule of thumb

A framework is a checklist of questions somebody learnt the hard way. **Use the questions; produce
only the documents somebody will read.** For each TOGAF deliverable, ask: *who reads this, and what
do they do differently because of it?* If there is no answer, skip it and write down that you did.
Lessons 9 and 10 apply the same rule to TOGAF's neighbours, IAF and Zachman.
