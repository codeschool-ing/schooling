The fourth audience is outside Faro. Ligeiro, the regional carrier, makes most of the deliveries, and
some of the fix may need its help. **An external audience changes three things: what you may share, how
you frame responsibility, and what you can ask for.**

## What you may share

Faro's cancellation rates, its margin and its customers' behaviour are its own business, and some of it
is commercially sensitive. A presentation to Ligeiro shares **what Ligeiro needs to act and nothing
more**: the share of first deliveries that are late, the dates, and the fact that the first delivery is
the one that matters most to Faro's customers. It does not share the R$ 790 thousand, which tells a
supplier exactly how much leverage it has in the next contract negotiation.

## Responsibility, framed honestly

Marina's analysis found that the delay is caused mostly by a step **inside Faro's warehouse**, the manual
address check, not by Ligeiro's drivers. That is good news for the relationship and it should be said
plainly: a client presentation that implied the carrier was to blame would be false and would poison the
conversation.

So the framing is a shared goal, written in the terms of the contract both companies signed:

> Our contract promises delivery within two working days. For first orders we are missing that 17.3% of
> the time, mostly because of a step on our side. We're changing it, and we'd like your help with two
> things that would make the change work.

## What you can ask for

An external audience owes you nothing beyond the contract, so **the request has to be specific and
cheap for them**. Faro's two: pick up first orders in the afternoon collection rather than the next
morning's, and send the delivered scans daily rather than weekly, so that the pilot can be measured as it
runs. Both are small, both are easy to say yes to, and both make Ligeiro part of a success rather than
the subject of a complaint.

## When the client is the one paying

If you work for a consultancy or an agency, the client is the company that hired you, and it is the most
important audience of all. Most of this section still applies: **share what serves their decision,
frame problems as things to fix rather than faults to assign, and ask for something specific**. What
changes is that the client's own board, management and technical team are three more rooms, and the
first three sections of this lesson apply to each.
