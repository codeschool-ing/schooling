---
title: When you are not sure, recommend a test
version: 1
---

Marina does not know that removing the check will work, or how many boxes will go astray without it.
**When the effect is uncertain and the change is reversible, the right recommendation is a test**: small,
time-boxed, with the result that would mean "go" decided before it starts.

## The parts of a pilot

- **Scope.** One region, the interior, because it has the highest late share (23.6%) and so the clearest
  signal.
- **Duration.** Eight weeks, from 1 September to 26 October, long enough for about 730 interior first
  orders at the first half's pace, and short enough that a failure costs little.
- **Success criterion, in advance.** Late first deliveries in the interior at 8% or less, measured weekly.
- **A guardrail.** A measure that must not get worse while the main one improves: boxes returned for a
  wrong address must stay under 1% of first orders. Faro chose that figure; the point is that it was chosen
  before the pilot, not after.
- **A comparison.** The capital keeps the manual check, so if late deliveries fall everywhere for some
  other reason, the comparison shows it.
- **A decision rule.** If the criterion is met and the guardrail holds, roll out to the capital; if the
  guardrail fails, keep the check for addresses the lookup cannot confirm; if neither moves, stop.

## Why not a proper experiment?

A randomised test, where each new order is assigned at random to keep or skip the check, would separate
the effect of the change from everything else more cleanly. The `bi-techniques` course covers how to
design one, in its lessons 7 to 10. Faro's warehouse runs one process per region, so a split by region is
what can be run; **say so in the recommendation**, and say what the comparison with the capital can and
cannot rule out.

## The lagging measure still matters

The pilot is judged on the leading indicator, late first deliveries, because it moves in weeks. Whether
cancellations actually fall will only be known ninety days after each cohort's first box, as lesson 7
explained. **The recommendation should say when that check happens** (early in 2026 for the September
cohort), so the claim at the root of the whole analysis gets tested rather than assumed.

A recommendation to test is not a weaker recommendation. It is the honest one when the evidence supports
trying and not yet committing, and it is far easier for a manager to approve.
