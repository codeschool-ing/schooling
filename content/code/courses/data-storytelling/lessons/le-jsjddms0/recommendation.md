title: Recommendation: where the story stops
version: 1
---

A story told with data **ends on what should happen next**, and it stops there. Not on a summary, not on
"questions?", not on a slide of thanks: on the decision you are asking the room to make.

## Faro's recommendation

Marina's analysis also found the likely cause. Every first order waits for somebody in the warehouse to
check the new address by hand, and on a promise of two working days that check costs one of them.
Renewals skip it, because the address is already known. So her recommendation is about that step:

> Run an eight-week pilot in the interior that removes the manual address check from first orders, with
> the aim of bringing late first deliveries there from 23.6% to 8%. Sandra's team owns it; we review
> in October.

It names **what** (remove one step for first orders), **where** (the interior, where the problem is
largest), **how much** (from 23.6%, the interior's rate, to 8%), **who** (Sandra's team) and **when** (eight weeks, reviewed in
October). Lesson 9 takes each of those apart; for now the point is that a recommendation without them is
an observation dressed as advice.

Notice also who owns it. Giving the pilot to Sandra's team, rather than to a new project, turns the
person whose number was challenged into the person who fixes the problem. Lesson 14 explains why that is
often the difference between a change that happens and one that is resisted.

## Why it comes last

The recommendation resolves the conflict, so **it can only land after the conflict has been felt and the
evidence has made it credible**. A room that has just seen that a late first box more than doubles
cancellations is ready to hear "fix the first box". The same sentence at minute one is a request with no
reason attached.

That seems to contradict the next lesson, which says to state your conclusion first. It does not, and
lesson 3 shows how: the conclusion is announced at the start in one sentence, and the recommendation is
argued for and asked for at the end. The structure here is the order of the argument; lesson 3 is about
not making people wait to know where it is going.

## A story with no recommendation

Sometimes the honest ending is not an action but a decision to find out more: the data cannot yet
separate two explanations. That is still a recommendation, and it should be just as specific: *run the
test that separates them, here is what it costs, here is when we will know*. **"Further analysis is
needed" with no owner and no date is the end of a story nobody will remember.**
