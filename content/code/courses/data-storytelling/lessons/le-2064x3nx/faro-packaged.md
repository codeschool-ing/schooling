If Marina were applying for a job with this work, and her employer allowed her to show it, her case study
would be a folder of a few files. **Each one is something this course already built.**

| file | what it is | from |
|---|---|---|
| `README` | the one-page summary, then the seven parts | lessons 8 and 16 |
| `faro.csv` | the twenty-four rows | lesson 1 |
| `faro.ods` | every number as a formula over the data | lessons 1, 9, 10 and 11 |
| `definitions` | what late, first delivery and cancelled mean | lesson 4 |
| `deck.pdf` | the presented deck, dated, with its appendix | lessons 5, 6 and 10 |
| `decisions` | the decision log | lesson 15 |

The order matters. **The summary is the first thing anybody opens**, the data and the spreadsheet are what a
sceptical reviewer opens second, and the deck is what shows the communication.

## The story it tells

Read in order, the folder tells the story of this course: a question from Paulo, a first attempt that
failed in a meeting, a second built from the decision backwards, a finding that survived the regional
check, a recommendation sized in money with a range, a pilot with its criterion written in advance, and a
result. The interior's late first deliveries fell from 23.6% to 7.1% in eight weeks, and the change went to
the capital in November. **The cancellation result is still open**, and saying so is part of the story.

## Confidentiality

Real analyses use real company data, and **most employers do not allow it to be published**, in a portfolio or
anywhere else. Before you show any work analysis, ask. If the answer is no, you have three options, in order
of preference:

- **Recreate it on public data** with the same shape, and say that you did.
- **Publish the method and the story with the numbers removed or transformed**, with the employer's
  permission, and say that you did.
- **Describe it in an interview without showing files.**

Personal data needs more care still. Brazil's data protection law, the LGPD, applies to anything that can
identify a customer, and the `data-governance` course covers it in its lesson 7. Faro's data is aggregated
and invented; yours may not be.
