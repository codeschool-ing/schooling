**The product page is the app's description, screenshots, keywords, category, age rating and price,
and App Review reads it as carefully as the app.** A listing that promises what the app does not do
is rejected under guideline 2.3, *Accurate Metadata*, however good the app is. Nothing here was run;
it is written from App Store Connect help on app information and screenshots, and from the
guidelines, read on 30 October 2026.

## Screenshots

Apple's screenshot specifications say that you "must include at least one screenshot and can upload
up to 10 screenshots per device size", and name the required size for iPhone as "iPhone with Dynamic
Island (medium display)". The same page says screenshots for the largest required size are scaled down
for the smaller ones when the interface is the same.

**A screenshot is the app in use, not an advertisement.** App Review's page on common issues asks that
screenshots "accurately communicate value and functionality". For the planner that is four pictures
of the four screens with a believable week in them: `CourseListView`, `CourseDetailView`,
`SessionForm` and `WeekView`, the week of 19 October with 180 minutes planned and 120 done.
The previews of lesson 20 section 07 are the quickest way to set up those states, and the simulator's
screenshot command takes the pictures.

## The words

- **Name and subtitle**: guideline 2.3.7 says that "app names must be limited to 30 characters",
  and the name has to be free in that language (section 02).
- **Description**: what the app does, plainly. Guideline 2.3.7 asks that metadata not be stuffed with
  terms for search; keywords have a field of their own.
- **Category and age rating**: the rating comes from a questionnaire in App Store Connect, not from
  your opinion of the app.
- **Support URL and privacy policy URL**: both required. App Review's common-issues page says "a link
  to user support with up-to-date contact information and a link to your privacy policy is required
  for all apps", and guideline 5.1.1(i) says what the policy has to contain.

**A localised listing is as many listings as languages.** The planner is written in English and
Portuguese in this course's examples, so its page is translated too, screenshots included, since a
Portuguese reader looking at English screens is reading about a different app.
