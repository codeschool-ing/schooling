---
title: Never anybody's real data
version: 1
---

Everything else in this lesson is a matter of doing it well. This part is a rule.

**Demo data is invented, always.** Not the real names from the job that inspired the project, not a
copy of a real spreadsheet with the names changed, not your colleagues' e-mail addresses in a screenshot.
Marta's IT room is fictional and so are Beatriz, Carlos, Dora and Eduardo, and loanbook would be the same
project if the real room had been down the corridor.

Three reasons, each sufficient. **It is not yours to publish.** People gave their names to a school to
borrow a projector, not to appear in somebody's portfolio. **The law agrees**: data-protection laws such as
Brazil's LGPD and Europe's GDPR govern personal data regardless of how small the project is. **And a
reviewer notices**: real-looking private data in a public repository reads as a lapse of judgement in
exactly the area a hiring manager cannot afford one.

Two places it hides. **In screenshots**: a browser tab, a bookmark bar or a notification can show an
e-mail or a name; take screenshots in a clean browser profile, or with a script, as this lesson did. **In the
history**: a real export committed once and deleted is still in the repository, lesson 14's lesson applied
to people instead of passwords.

Invented data has one more advantage: you can shape it. Real data rarely contains exactly one overdue loan
at the moment you need a screenshot.
