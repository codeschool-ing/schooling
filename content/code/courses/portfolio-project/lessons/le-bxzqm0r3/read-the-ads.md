---
title: Reading job adverts for verbs
version: 1
---

Whatever your track, you can check the section above against the market you are actually applying to.
It takes an evening and a text file.

**Collect ten adverts** for the job you want, at the level you are: junior, trainee, first job. Real
ones, current, in the city or the remote market you are aiming at.

**Copy out two lists from each.** The *nouns* are the tools: Python, Kubernetes, Power BI. The *verbs*
are the tasks: *maintain* the integration with the payment provider, *investigate* incidents,
*document* procedures, *build* dashboards for the sales team.

**Count them.** The nouns tell you what to mention; they vary from one company to the next, and a
project cannot cover them all. The verbs are what the project should *do*, and they repeat: across ten
adverts for the same role the same five or six tasks come back in different words.

Then **build the project around the verbs that repeat most**, using whichever of the nouns fits
naturally. A back-end advert that says *design and maintain REST APIs* and *write automated tests* six
times out of ten is describing loanbook's shape more precisely than any list of frameworks.

Two cautions. Adverts ask for more than any junior has, so do not read them as a checklist to
complete. And keep the file: lesson 19's two-minute presentation uses the same verbs.
