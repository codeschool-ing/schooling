---
title: Fifteen minutes a week
version: 1
---

Between milestones, one habit keeps a project honest: **a short written review, once a week, at the
same time**. Fifteen minutes, three questions, in a file in the repository or a note on the board of
lesson 8.

```localised
week 3 — 19 June
shipped:  loans are marked late (v0.2.0 on the 15th); labels on every field
next:     the container file; a first deploy on the lab server
cut:      the search box moves to could, it will not make v1.0.0
worry:    I have not tried HTTPS yet and do not know how long it takes
```

**Shipped** is what exists now that did not last week: things that run, not things started. If the
answer is *nothing*, the week's problem is found while it is one week old. **Next** is the one or two
tasks for the coming week, from lesson 5's list. **Cut** is the decision nobody else will make for you,
written down on the day it is made.

The fourth line is optional and the most useful. **A worry named early is a task; a worry found late
is a missed milestone.** The note above was right to worry: the milestone with the deploy in it was the
longest gap on the timeline, two weeks.

These notes cost nothing to keep, and at the end they are the raw material for lesson 21's
retrospective. Written weekly, they are what happened; written from memory in July, they are what you
would like to have happened.
