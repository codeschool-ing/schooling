**To do** holds the current milestone's tasks and anything noticed along the way, each card marked
with the milestone it belongs to. **Doing** holds what you are working on. **Done** holds what is
finished, meaning committed and working, not *mostly there*.

The one rule is on the middle column: **one card in doing at a time**, two at most. This is called a
*work-in-progress limit*, and for a single person it is the whole of project management. Two cards in
doing means one is waiting while you work on the other, and a card waiting in *doing* is a task you
have started and not finished, which is exactly what lesson 7's milestones cannot absorb. When
something urgent arrives, the card in doing goes back to *to do*, visibly, instead of both being
half done.

Two small habits keep the board honest. **Move a card the moment its state changes**, not at the end
of the week, or the board describes last Tuesday. And **empty *done* at each milestone**, into the
weekly note of lesson 7: the finished cards are the *shipped* line, and a board that starts each
milestone empty on the right is a board you can read at a glance.
