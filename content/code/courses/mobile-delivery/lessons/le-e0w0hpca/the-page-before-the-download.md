**Everything a person knows about the planner before installing it is on one page, and they spend
seconds on it.** Lesson 4 wrote that page as files; this section is about what it does to the
decision. *Read from the stores' documentation for developers, October 2026; neither console was
opened.*

## What the person sees, roughly in order

1. **The icon, the name and the subtitle** or short description, in a search result, often before
   the page itself.
2. **The rating and the number of ratings.** A rating of 4.6 from twelve people and 4.4 from twelve
   thousand are read differently, and both stores show the count beside it.
3. **The first two or three screenshots**, lesson 4's most valuable pictures.
4. **The size**, the subject of the last two sections, shown on the page and, on Android, again in
   the install button's flow when the download is large.
5. **The privacy summary**, lesson 5's label, which a growing share of people do read.
6. **The description**, which a minority reads, and the reviews, read when the rest left doubt.

## The number this page produces

**Install conversion** is the share of people who saw the page and installed: page visitors in,
installs out. It is the one number that measures the page itself, and both stores report it: the Play
Console as the store listing's conversion rate, App Store Connect as the conversion rate of product
page views to first downloads.

## Testing the page

Both stores can show different versions of the page to different visitors and count the installs:

| | Google Play | App Store |
|---|---|---|
| name | **store listing experiments** | **product page optimisation** |
| what can vary | icon, screenshots, short and full description, feature graphic | icon, screenshots, app previews |
| how it decides | the console reports the difference with a confidence range | the console reports the improvement with a confidence level |

Apple also offers **custom product pages**: separate versions of the page, each with its own address,
for a campaign or an audience. The planner could have one for students preparing for the ENEM, linked
from where they gather.

The next section does by hand what those consoles do, so the confidence range they print means
something when you read it.
