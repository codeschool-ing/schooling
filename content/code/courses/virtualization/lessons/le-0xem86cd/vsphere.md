The whole family is called **vSphere**, and the words you will hear from its administrators are these:

| word | what it is |
|---|---|
| host | one server running ESXi |
| datastore | where disks live: VMFS on the host's or a shared disk, or an NFS share |
| vSwitch, port group | the host's virtual switch, and a named group of its ports with a VLAN |
| vMotion | moving a running VM to another host, which needs vCenter |
| HA | restarting a failed host's VMs on the others |
| VMware Tools | the guest agent, lesson 3 |
