---
title: What a data mart is
version: 1
---

A **data mart** is a part of the warehouse cut to fit one group of people: the sales team's tables, finance's
tables, marketing's. It holds the facts and dimensions that group asks about, under names it recognises, and
leaves out the rest. A mart is about who reads it, not about how it is built, and that is why the word covers
two arrangements that behave very differently.

- **A dependent mart** is built from the warehouse. Its facts come from the warehouse's fact tables and its
  dimensions are the warehouse's dimensions, filtered or renamed. It can be a set of views, a schema of
  summary tables, or a copy in a separate database for performance. Whatever its form, it says nothing the
  warehouse does not say.
- **An independent mart** is built straight from the sources, by the group that wants it, with no warehouse
  underneath. It is quicker to start: one team, one question, one load. It is also a second definition of
  everything it touches.

Inmon's architecture of lesson 6 ends in dependent marts: the normalised warehouse is the truth, and every
mart is cut from it. Kimball's builds the warehouse out of the marts themselves, one business process at a
time, held together by the conformed dimensions of lesson 3. Either way **the marts share their dimensions**,
and that is what keeps them dependent.

Independent marts are how most companies actually start. A team needs a report, nobody owns a warehouse, and a
spreadsheet becomes a database. The next three sections build one dependent mart and two independent ones over
the same December, and ask them all the same question.
