**A distribution is the shape of a column: where its values gather, how far they spread, and what
lies at the edges.** A mean says one of those three things. A chart says all of them, and the right
chart depends on which you need to compare.

Trip lengths have a long right tail: most trips are short, a few are very long. The numbers first:

```python
trips.groupby("plan")["minutes"].quantile([0.5, 0.9, 0.99]).round(1).unstack()
```

```
        0.50  0.90  0.99
plan                    
annual  12.4  23.8  40.1
day     19.9  38.3  65.4
```

Half of the subscribers' trips are under 12.4 minutes, nine in ten under 23.8, and one in a hundred
over 40. The day passes reach 65 minutes at the same point. **The 99th percentile is three times the
median** for both plans, which is what a long tail means.

The box runs from the first quartile to the third, so it holds the middle half of the trips, and the
line inside it is the median. The whiskers reach the furthest trip within one and a half box-lengths
of the box, and every trip beyond them is drawn as a separate point. Those points are what a box plot
calls **outliers**, and there are many:

```python
def beyond_whiskers(m):
    q1, q3 = m.quantile([0.25, 0.75])
    return int((m > q3 + 1.5 * (q3 - q1)).sum())

trips.groupby("plan")["minutes"].agg(beyond_whiskers)
```

```
plan
annual    843
day       364
Name: minutes, dtype: int64
```

Hundreds of trips per plan are "outliers" by that rule, and none of them is an error: a long tail is
a property of the data, and the generator of lesson 8 draws lengths from a distribution that has one.
**A point beyond a whisker is a value to look at, not a value to delete.** Deleting them would remove
the longest trips from every later calculation, and the 99th percentile above would quietly shrink.

The lost bikes appear in neither chart. Their `minutes` are `NaN`, and seaborn, like pandas, leaves
missing values out without saying so; 146 trips are missing from both charts, which for a trip-length
chart is the right answer and is still worth knowing.
