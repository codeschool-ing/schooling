## Ageing

**None.** ARIMA and A/B testing predate everybody, and lessons 16 to 19 are an overview rather than a tool.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The A/B testing half is the best-graded material in the category after `statistics` itself.** Sample size, minimum detectable effect, significance, peeking — every one is a number with a right answer, gradeable by `numeric` today. Lessons 7 to 11 could be written and shipped with nothing new built.

**2 ·** **Lessons 16 to 19 are `machine-learning` in overview, and the overlap needs a decision.** Supervised and unsupervised learning, classification and regression, algorithmic bias — all four are lessons in `machine-learning` at depth. `bi` reaches this course and never reaches `machine-learning`; `data-science` reaches both, this one at position 9 and that one at 8. **So a `data-science` student meets the overview after the real thing**, which is the wrong order and is invisible from inside either course.

**3 ·** **Lesson 18 and 19 are ethics, and they are judgement.** Same grading problem `management` has, in two lessons rather than twenty-four — small enough to solve with scenario questions without it dominating the course.
