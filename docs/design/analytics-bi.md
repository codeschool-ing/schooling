## Ageing

**Severe.** Lesson 4 is Power BI, lesson 5 is four more products, lesson 8 is three SaaS vendors. **Eight of ten lessons show somebody else's interface**, which is the worst ratio in the category outside the vendor family.

## Flags

**1 ·** **Ten lessons for sixty hours — 12.9 sections each, the widest in the entire sweep.** Lesson 5 alone is Tableau, Looker, Metabase and Streamlit. This is not a lesson list, it is a syllabus of four courses, and pretending otherwise leaves two thirds of the hours unaccounted for.

**2 ·** **Power BI is a Windows desktop application**, which puts this course in the same class as `excel-analytics` and `operating-systems`: material whose environment is a licensed product on an operating system the platform cannot hand out. Metabase and Streamlit are open source and self-hostable, so **there is a version of this course built on those two** — smaller reach in the job market, and actually teachable.

**3 ·** **Its last lesson is `data-storytelling`'s whole course.** *"Telling stories with data without misleading"* is lesson 10 here and 50 hours there, and both are in `bi` — at positions 8 and 15. Not a contradiction, but the second time in the sweep that a lesson duplicates a later course, and whoever writes the pair should decide which one owns it.
