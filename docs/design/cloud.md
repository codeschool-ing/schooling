## Flags

**1 ·** **Ten lessons for sixty hours — 12.9 sections each, the widest in the category.** These are not lessons in the sense `kubernetes` uses the word; each is closer to a small course. That is a design instruction rather than a defect: a lesson this wide needs its own internal arc, and treating it like a 4-section lesson would leave two thirds of the hours unaccounted for.

**2 ·** **The hub of the category.** Eight tracks, six dependents, and the only course the three vendor families all sit behind. What is vague here is vague in nine other courses.
