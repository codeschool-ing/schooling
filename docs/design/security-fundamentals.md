## Flags

**1 ·** **Free, and this is what `C-28` was written for.** Position 1 of `security`, a track that continues nothing, so a visitor really can arrive here having paid for nothing. Set against `process-management`, `architecture-role` and `bigdata` — free by the same rule and reached only after a whole paid track — **this is the working case, and having all four in the register is what shows the rule is fine and its blindness to `continues` is the defect.**

**2 ·** **Sixty free hours that need no environment at all.** No runtime, no browser, no database, no target, no account. With `statistics` and `data-fundamentals` it is one of a very small set that could be written and shipped against what exists today — and unlike those two it is already the free sample of a track, which makes it the most immediately useful of the three.

**3 ·** **Lesson 18 is a careers lesson inside a fundamentals course, and it is placed right.** SOC, pentest, GRC, forensics and AppSec map onto `soc-response`, `pentest`, `threat-modeling` and `secure-code` — the track's own later courses. It is the map of the track, told from position 1, and it should be written as that rather than as a list of job titles.
