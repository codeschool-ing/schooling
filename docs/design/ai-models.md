## Ageing

**The most severe in the catalogue, and it is not close.** Fifteen of twenty-one lessons name a specific product: Claude, Gemini, the GPT and o families, Cohere, Mistral, Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemma, Hugging Face, Transformers.js, Ollama, LM Studio, OpenRouter, and four APIs by name. **This is not a console being redrawn — these are products that get discontinued.**

## Flags

**1 ·** **It is a catalogue of a market, and a market is the one thing a course cannot be.** Six lessons — 6 to 11 — are one model family each, and the list was already changing while it was being written. Recorded as video under `C-30`, where the screen is the frame, **this course has a re-recording cost that recurs on somebody else's schedule and never stops.**

The design instruction that follows is specific: **split the durable spine from the catalogue.** Lessons 1 to 5 and 21 — what a pretrained model is, open against closed, when self-hosting pays, how to choose, how to evaluate against your own cases, and spend control — are permanent and deserve the full treatment. Lessons 6 to 20 are a directory, and a directory should be **text-first, short, and cheap to re-render**, which is the opposite of what a lesson list of this length invites.

**2 ·** **Lesson 5 is the one that outlives the rest and is easy to under-write.** *"Evaluating models against your own set of cases"* is the skill that survives every product on the list being replaced, and it is one lesson among fifteen that name vendors.

**3 ·** **Two dependents and one track.** `embeddings-vectors` and `multimodal` both require it, and only `ai`(6) reaches it. Narrow reach, high ageing, two courses downstream — which argues for writing the spine early and the directory as late as possible.
