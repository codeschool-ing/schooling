## Flags

**1 ·** **Forty lessons at 2.00 hours each — joint second-most granular in the catalogue** with `java`, behind `kubernetes` (48). The budget puts it at 4.3 sections a lesson, on the floor, and that is right: *"Zero values, `const`, `iota`, scope and shadowing"* is one lesson and does not want nine sections.

**2 ·** **It opens the most expensive path in the catalogue.** `go` → `go-concurrency` → `go-back` → `go-production` is **290 hours**, against 140 for the JavaScript path in the same fork. See the register: the four options of `backend`'s language choice are not comparable and the track presents them as though they were.

**3 ·** **Lessons 30 and 31 are generics, and they date the course.** They arrived in 2022 and the material has to teach them as ordinary rather than as news — which is the trap a course written now and read in five years falls into.
