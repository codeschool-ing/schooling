## Flags

**1 ·** **This course can be taught and `ios-apps` cannot, and lesson 20 is why.** *"Where Swift runs besides the iPhone: macOS, server and Linux"* — the Swift toolchain runs on Linux, so a sandbox can compile and test everything in these twenty lessons. **The wall is Xcode, not Swift**, and it falls between this course and its dependent rather than across both.

**2 ·** **Which makes the split worth designing to.** If the iOS path is ever published knowing its second half cannot be practised, this half still can be — and saying so in the material is better than a student discovering it at lesson 21 of the next course.

**3 ·** **Twenty lessons at 8.6 sections each**, matching `kotlin` exactly. The two platform languages were designed as a pair, which is defensible and worth confirming rather than assuming.
