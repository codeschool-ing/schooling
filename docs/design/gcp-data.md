## Ageing

**Severe.** Twenty-six of the lessons name a product of Google Cloud's, and products get renamed, repriced and redrawn on their owner's schedule. This is the material `VIDEO.md` says needs a new video version with no change to its script.

## Flags

**1 ·** **Three more vendor courses, and the catalogue now has twelve.** The `infra` sweep found nine — the `aws-`, `azure-` and `gcp-foundations`, `-compute` and `-operations` families. These three bring it to twelve courses, **750 hours**, blocked by a bill rather than by engineering. That is no longer a note on a category; it is the single largest class of unbuildable material in the catalogue.

**2 ·** **Reached only through a choice, so nothing after it may assume it.** The `data` track forks at position 13 into one of the three, and positions 14 onward — `ai-dev`, the portfolio, `first-job` — are written for a student who took exactly one and cannot be told which.

**3 ·** **The bill is the lesson in a way the `infra` family never was.** Lesson 5 is egress and request charges, lesson 19 is "the two decisions that define the bill", the last lesson chooses a store on price among other things. A course that teaches cost while a student cannot incur any is teaching the one thing that has to be felt. Whatever sandbox story the vendor courses get, this family argues for a *sandbox account with a real invoice on it* rather than a simulator.
