## Flags

**1 ·** **Both stores charge before a student can practise any of it.** A Play Console account and an Apple developer account are annual fees, and lesson 3 teaches exactly that. This is the `aws-`/`azure-`/`gcp-` problem in a category nobody had counted it in: **fourteen courses in the catalogue are now blocked by somebody else's bill**, and this is the fifteenth if both platforms count.

**2 ·** **Lesson 10 is the iOS wall again, named plainly** — *"CI for mobile: a hosted Mac, caching, and signing inside the pipeline"*. The course assumes the workaround `react-native` names and `ios-apps` does not.

**3 ·** **Lessons 15 to 19 are analytics and product measurement**, which is `delivery-metrics` and `analytics-bi` material seen from a mobile app. Lesson 16 draws a line — *"Analytics: events, funnels, and measurement that is not surveillance"* — that neither of those two courses draws. **The narrowest course says the thing the two broad ones do not**, which is worth keeping when the material is written.
