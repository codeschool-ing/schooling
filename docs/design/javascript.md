## Ageing

**Low.** ES6 is a decade old and settled. Lesson 21 names three package managers and is the one that moves.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The only course that is both a language and the entry to two fork options.** `backend`'s JavaScript path and `mobile`'s React Native path both start here, plus `frontend` in sequence and `devops`/`devsecops` as an automation language. That is why it reaches six tracks while every other language course except `python` reaches one or three.

**2 ·** **Lessons 11 to 13 are the browser and the rest is the language**, which matters because `node` — its own dependent — needs the language half and none of the DOM. The material should keep that seam visible rather than interleaving.

**3 ·** **Twenty-two lessons at 7.8 sections each**, against `go` and `java` at 40 lessons and 4.3. Three beginner language courses, two of them twice as granular as the third — **that is a difference in design rather than in subject**, and it is worth deciding deliberately rather than inheriting.
