## Reach

In **3 tracks** — `bi`, `dba`, `it-support` — at position 1 in all three, so it is free in all
three by `C-28`. Reached directly; never behind a choice.

**Depends on it:** `bi-business`, `excel-analytics`.

## Assumes, and leaves ready

**Assumes nothing.** It is the first course of the school for anybody arriving through those three
tracks.

**Leaves ready:** little. Two dependents, both about spreadsheets. This course carries no
obligation to set up the ones below it, which is unusual for something this size and is the reason
it can be designed without reference to anything else.

## Ageing

**Yes, and more than any other course in this category.** Lessons 9 to 11 are the Microsoft Office
suite, Google Workspace, and iCloud/OneDrive/Drive. Those interfaces change on their vendors'
schedules, so any screen recorded from them is wrong while the script is still right — the one
failure `VIDEO.md` names as needing a new video version with no text change. Whatever can be
taught from an annotated still rather than a recording should be.

## Flags

**1 · Sixty hours, free — the free-tier decision in concrete form.** If the shop window narrows to
`web-fundamentals` alone, this course becomes paid and its video budget stops being bounded by
unmetered egress. **The sheet does not choose**; it records that the number waits on a decision
nobody has taken.

**2 · Not blocked by the sandbox, and blocked by illustration.** ~85 diagrams against zero
impossible exercises. It is the largest bottleneck in `foundations` and it is of a different kind
entirely — nobody has been named to draw them, in this course or in any other.
