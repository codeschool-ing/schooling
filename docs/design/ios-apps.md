## Ageing

**Severe.** SwiftUI and SwiftData both move yearly with iOS, and lesson 22 is App Store review, whose rules change without notice.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The fourth course in the catalogue with no path from a browser at any price** — after `operating-systems`, `virtualization` and `api-mobile-automation`'s iOS lessons. Xcode is macOS-only by Apple's licence, the simulator with it, and lessons 21 and 22 need a paid developer account on top.

**And this one is worse than the other three, because it is a whole fork option.** `mobile` offers three paths at exactly 170 hours each — Android, iOS, React Native — presented as equals. One of them cannot be practised. **The hours are equal and the buildability is not**, and nothing in the track says so.

**2 ·** **Its prerequisite is fine and that is the useful part.** `swift` runs on Linux — the course says so in its own lesson 20 — so the first 80 hours of the iOS path are teachable and only the second 90 are not. **The wall falls between the two courses, not across both.**

**3 ·** **`react-native` names the workaround in its own lesson 20** — *"EAS Build: credentials, and building without a Mac in the room"*. A hosted Mac build service is what `mobile-delivery` lesson 10 also assumes. So the catalogue already knows the answer for *building*; what it does not solve is a student *developing* against a simulator.
