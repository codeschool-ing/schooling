## Flags

**1 ·** **Half of it is money, and `numeric` grades money.** Total cost of ownership, cost of delay, debt priced as interest per sprint, the engineering budget — those are arithmetic with a right answer and a tolerance. This course is much more gradeable than its category average, and it sits at the end of the track where the student is most likely to have stopped. Worth building early for that reason rather than for its reach, which is one track.

**2 ·** **Lesson 1 refuses the thing most readers came for.** "A strategy is a diagnosis, a guiding policy and coherent action — not a list of goals" is Rumelt, and it is a definition that contradicts how the word is used in most companies. The material has to earn it in the first lesson or the nineteen after it are read as a list of goals anyway.

**3 ·** **Twenty lessons in fifty hours — 5.3 sections a lesson**, the thin end of `management`. These are short, sharp lessons rather than the wide ones `process-management` carries, and the section design should not fight that: an opening, two readings and a drill is the shape the hours buy.
