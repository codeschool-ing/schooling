## Flags

**1 ·** **Lessons 18 and 19 need a Mac, and that is a wall rather than a cost.** XCUITest and SwiftTesting run on macOS and nowhere else; an iOS simulator is macOS-only by Apple's licence. **This is the third course in the catalogue with no path from a browser at any price** — after `operating-systems` and `virtualization` — and unlike those two the blocked part is only about a fifth of the course. **Declare it**: the honest design is an Android and API course that describes the iOS half from the outside and says so.

**2 ·** **It is two courses in one file, and the seam is at lesson 14.** Lessons 1 to 13 are API testing; 14 to 22 are mobile. They share a track position and nothing else — different tools, different environments, different jobs. **The section design has to carry a hinge at 14**, and pretending it is one continuous subject is the failure mode.

**3 ·** **Lesson 20 is device clouds — a paid SaaS — and lesson 19 says the emulator lies.** So the course names both the cheap option and its limits, honestly, which is the right treatment and the one to keep when the material is written.
