# gcp-foundations

**Google Cloud: Foundations, Identity and Network** · `co-3yzpppw9` · 60 h declared · intermediate · 26 lessons · `infra` · paid

## Reach

In **3 tracks** — `cloud-engineering`(9, escolha *Google Cloud*), `data`(13, escolha *Google Cloud*), `devops`(10, escolha *Google Cloud*).

**Reached through a choice**, never in sequence — so **no course after that fork may assume it was taken**.

**Depends on it:** `gcp-compute`, `gcp-data`

## Assumes, and leaves ready

**Assumes:** `cloud` — the vocabulary of providers, managed services and serverless. It does not re-explain what a region is.

**Leaves ready:** the account model, identity, billing and networking of Google Cloud, for `gcp-compute` and `gcp-data`.

## Ageing

**Severe.** The screen is Google Cloud's console, and a console changes on its owner's schedule — the one kind of material that goes wrong while its script stays right. Whatever can be taught from an annotated still rather than a recording should be, and what cannot is declared per lesson.

## Flags

**1 ·** **It has to stand alone, and two of its three tracks prove it.** `cloud-engineering` continues into `gcp-compute` and `gcp-operations`; `data` continues into `gcp-data`; `devops` stops here. So this course cannot be written as the first third of a trilogy — it is the whole of the vendor for one track out of three.

**2 ·** **Nothing after the choice may assume it.** The three vendors are options of one fork, so a student arrives having done exactly one. No later course in any of those tracks can take a vendor for granted.

**3 ·** **One design, three instances.** `aws-`, `azure-` and `gcp-foundations` have the same lesson count and titles that match word for word. They are one shape applied three times, and writing them apart would produce three.
