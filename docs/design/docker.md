In **6 tracks** — `backend`(11), `cloud-engineering`(5), `data`(12), `dba`(9), `devops`(7), `devsecops`(10).

**Depends on it:** `architecture`, `aws-compute`, `azure-compute`, `gcp-compute`, `kubernetes`

## Assumes, and leaves ready

**Assumes:** `linux-terminal` — the shell, processes, filesystem and permissions.

**Leaves ready:** images, containers, volumes and networking, for `kubernetes`, the three `*-compute` courses and `architecture`. **Nothing downstream re-explains what a container is.**

## Ageing

**Low.** The CLI has been stable for years; Docker Desktop's interface has not, so anything taught from that window is the exception rather than the rule.

## Flags

**1 ·** **Twenty-eight lessons in fifty hours puts it on the floor: 4 sections a lesson.** There is no room to give a lesson a second reading, so either the lessons are finer than the rest of the catalogue or the fifty hours are low. It is the second course, after `kubernetes`, where the lesson count and the hours disagree.

**2 ·** **The heaviest sandbox ask in the category that a sandbox can actually meet.** A daemon is reachable; a cluster and a hypervisor are not. If anything after `shell` is built, this is the course that argues for it.
