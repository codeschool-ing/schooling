## Ageing

**None.** STRIDE is from the 1990s and the frameworks it names revise on multi-year cycles.

## Flags

**1 ·** **It needs no environment, and it is the second course in the catalogue whose exercises the platform cannot receive.** A student asked to model a system produces a **diagram**, and there is no answer type for one — no upload, no canvas, no comparison. `architecture-modeling` has exactly this problem and the same rescue: **`labelling` on a finished diagram** — "mark the trust boundary that is missing", "which STRIDE category is this" — which grades today and teaches, but never asks the student to draw.

Two courses, in two categories, blocked on the same missing answer type. That is now a decision with two courses behind it rather than one course's complaint.

**2 ·** **Fifty hours, no runtime, no target, no account — and ~90 diagrams.** It is fully unblocked on every axis the sweep tracks except the one nobody owns. **The illustration bill is the entire cost of this course**, which makes it an unusually clean test of whether that bill gets paid.

**3 ·** **One track only, and it gates `secure-pipeline`.** `devsecops`(9), nothing else. The narrowest reach of any course in `security` — and the fifth link of the catalogue's deepest chain, so its narrowness is structural rather than incidental.
