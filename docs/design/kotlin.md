## Flags

**1 ·** **Twenty lessons at 8.6 sections each — twice the width of `go` and `java` at the same level.** Three beginner language courses in this batch, and `kotlin` and `swift` are shaped like the frontend framework courses while `go` and `java` are shaped like reference manuals. **Neither shape is wrong and the catalogue should not hold both by accident.**

**2 ·** **Lesson 20 is the honest one** — *"Kotlin beyond Android: server, multiplatform and scripting"* — in a course reached only through the Android path. It tells a student their language is not a single-platform bet, which is the same service the frontend courses' final lessons perform.

**3 ·** **Half of a 170-hour fork option, and the fork's three options are exactly equal in hours** — unlike `backend`'s. `mobile` gets that right; its problem is elsewhere, and it is `ios-apps`.
