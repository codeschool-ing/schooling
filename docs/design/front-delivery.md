## Flags

**2 ·** **GraphQL is here and in `apis`**, which is `backend`(7). No `frontend` student reaches `apis`, so this is correct placement — eighth compressed overview, and the one where the two treatments are furthest apart in depth.

**3 ·** **The free-tier hosting of lesson 1 is the same material `portfolio-project` lesson 15 teaches**, and `frontend` reaches both — this at position 9 and the portfolio at 11. **Two courses, two positions apart, teaching deployment to a free tier.** Worth one of them deferring to the other explicitly.
