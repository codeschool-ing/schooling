## Ageing

**None.** Twenty-four lessons and not one screen belonging to somebody else. This is the cleanest course in the catalogue for `C-30`: everything it shows, we draw.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The hardest course in the catalogue to grade, and the sweep should say so plainly.** Twenty-four lessons on one-to-ones, feedback, promotion cases, underperformance and letting somebody go. `internal/grade` carries eight graders and every one of them checks a closed form — a choice, an ordering, a number, a label on a picture. **None of them reads a paragraph**, and a paragraph is what this material is made of.

**2 ·** **What rescues it is the scenario with four replies**, and that is a cost rather than a solution. "Here is what the engineer said; which of these four answers is the feedback and which three are judgement dressed as feedback" is a `quiz`, and it is gradeable. It is also several times more expensive to write than a quiz about a fact, because the three wrong answers have to be wrong for a reason a reader can find. At ~600 exercises, that is the largest authoring bill in these three categories and it buys the least reusable material.

**3 ·** **Nothing depends on it and one track reaches it.** Of the 780 hours swept here this is the least connected 60 — which is an argument for writing it late, not for writing it thin: it is the second course of `tech-lead` and a leadership track without the people part is not one.
