## Flags

**1 ·** **Eleven lessons for sixty hours — 11.7 sections each.** Lesson 2 is Next, Nuxt, SvelteKit and Angular SSR in one lesson: **four meta-frameworks, one per fork option**, which is the course discharging its obligation to all four readers in a single lesson. Correct, and it makes that lesson the widest in the course by a distance.

**2 ·** **Core Web Vitals is a metric somebody else owns.** INP replaced FID in 2024 and the thresholds move; a lesson taught as numbers dates faster than one taught as what the numbers measure. **The design instruction is to teach the perception and cite the thresholds**, not the reverse.

**3 ·** **Lessons 9 to 11 are `servers-cache` from the browser's side** — cache headers, CDN, service workers. `frontend` reaches neither `servers-cache` nor `linux-terminal`, so this is the only exposure. Seventh compressed overview.
