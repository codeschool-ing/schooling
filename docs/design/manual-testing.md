## Flags

**1 ·** **The best-graded course in the category, and the reason is that its subject is written artefacts.** A test case is text with a fixed shape; a defect report is text with required fields. "Which of these four is a boundary value", "order these steps so somebody else can run them", "what is missing from this defect report" — `quiz`, `ordering` and `cloze` on their natural material, no environment needed. **Roughly half of it is publishable against the graders that exist.**

**2 ·** **Lesson 3 is the skill the whole track is judged on** — *"Writing a case someone else can run without asking a single question"* — and it is the one thing here that a machine cannot grade, because the test is whether a stranger could follow it. It is also the most valuable lesson in the course. **That tension should be stated in the material rather than hidden behind a quiz about case design.**

**3 ·** **Four of twenty-two lessons are a product directory**, which is the `ai-models` shape at smaller scale and takes the same treatment: text-first, short, cheap to re-render, and not the place the video budget goes.
