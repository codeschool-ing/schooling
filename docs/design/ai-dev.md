## Flags

**1 ·** **Twelve tracks — the widest reach in the catalogue after `portfolio-project`, `first-job` (16) and `git` (13), tied with `web-fundamentals`.** In eleven of them it is the only AI course there is, and it is placed correctly: late, standalone, for somebody whose track is about something else.

**2 ·** **In the twelfth it is a summary of the four courses immediately before it.** `ai` reaches it at position 10, after `ai-models`(6), `embeddings-vectors`(7), `rag`(8) and `agents-mcp`(9). Its eleven lessons are tokens and embeddings, RAG, agents and MCP, function calling, providers, and risks — **a 50-hour compression of the 270 hours the student has just finished**. That is the same defect found in `data` (`bi-techniques`'s overview after `machine-learning`) and it is larger here.

**And it cannot be fixed inside the course**, because nothing in `content/` lets a course differ by the track that reached it. **This is the third instance** — `design-patterns` wants it for four languages, `portfolio-project` for sixteen tracks, and this one for the difference between being the only AI course and being the last of five. Three courses, three unrelated reasons, one missing mechanism.

**3 ·** **The cheapest thing that would help costs nothing to build**: the `ai` track could reach it before the deep courses rather than after. It is a position in a track file, not a mechanism — and the sheet raises it because a course reading as redundant to the students who studied hardest is worse than it sounds.
