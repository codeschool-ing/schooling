## Flags

**1 ·** **Thirteen lessons for seventy hours — 11.5 sections each.** Lesson 9 alone is OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect and SAML. Among the widest in the catalogue and it needs the internal arc treatment.

**2 ·** **It is the first course after the language fork and it names no language.** That is exactly right and it is the constraint the whole second half of `backend` lives under: a student arrives holding one of four languages and the material may not assume which. **`apis`, `architecture`, `scale`, `servers-cache` and `testing-cicd` are all written to that constraint** — worth recording as the catalogue solving a problem rather than raising one.

**3 ·** **Half of it is authentication and authorisation** — lessons 7 to 11 — which overlaps `secure-code` (sessions, passwords, access control) and `cryptography` (hashing). `backend` reaches neither, so this is the only exposure, and it is correctly placed. Fifth instance of a compressed overview.
