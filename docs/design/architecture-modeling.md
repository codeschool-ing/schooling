## Ageing

**Low.** UML is frozen, C4 is stable, TOGAF and Zachman have outlived several fashions. Lesson 12 names Mermaid, PlantUML and Structurizr, and those move — one lesson.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The course whose exercises the platform cannot receive.** Eight graders exist and all eight check a closed form. A student asked to model a system produces a diagram, and there is no answer type for it — no upload, no canvas, no comparison. **`labelling` is the whole rescue**: give the student a finished diagram and ask what is wrong with it, which grades and teaches, but never asks them to draw. A course on modelling in which the student never models is a real compromise and should be recorded as one rather than discovered later.

**2 ·** **Lesson 12 is a door out of flag 1 and nobody has walked through it.** Diagrams as code — Mermaid, PlantUML, Structurizr — is text, and text is gradeable: a student writes Mermaid, a renderer draws it, a comparison checks it. That is one lesson's subject and it could be the course's assessment mechanism throughout. It needs a renderer in the platform, which does not exist and is far cheaper than a sandbox.

**3 ·** **Fourteen lessons in sixty hours — 9.2 sections each, the widest of the fourteen swept.** Lesson 2 alone is five UML diagram types. These are not lessons in the sense `first-job` uses the word, and the section design must give each an internal arc or two thirds of the hours are unaccounted for.
