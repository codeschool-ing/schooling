## Flags

**1 ·** **Ninety hours, and it is the fork option that can actually be built.** An emulator is heavy — but it runs on Linux, in a container, without anybody buying hardware. `ios-apps` cannot say that at any price, and the two courses are presented to the student as equal choices.

**2 ·** **Twenty-two lessons and nearly every one names a library.** Compose, Material 3, Hilt, Retrofit, OkHttp, kotlinx.serialization, Moshi, Room, Coil, WorkManager. It is the `java-back` shape: the ecosystem *is* the course, so the directory treatment applies to most of it and the durable spine is lessons 3 to 5 and 10 — recomposition, state and one-way data flow.

**3 ·** **Half of a 170-hour fork option**, one track, nothing downstream.
