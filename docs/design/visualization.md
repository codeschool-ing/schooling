## Flags

**1 ·** **The second course whose images are the exercise, and with `data-storytelling` it makes a pair.** ~110 here and ~90 there, on the same subject, in the same two tracks, adjacent in both. **Two hundred images across two courses that must not contradict each other** — and no sheet before this one had two courses sharing an illustration budget.

**2 ·** **`labelling` is the native grader and it is already built.** "Mark what is wrong with this chart" grades today with no environment at all. Between this course and `data-storytelling`, `labelling` carries more assessment weight than anywhere else in the catalogue, and it is one of the eight graders that exist — **which makes these two courses unusually buildable for their size, once somebody draws.**

**3 ·** **Lesson 14 is accessibility and it is the platform's own problem too.** Colour blindness, contrast, never relying on colour alone — `tools/a11y-test` runs in this repository against the student interface. A course teaching the rule while the platform is checked against it is a coherence worth using rather than a coincidence.
