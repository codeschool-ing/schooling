## Flags

**1 ·** **The best-graded course in the catalogue, and it is not close.** `numeric` and `expression-answer` are two of the eight graders that exist, and this is the one course whose natural answers *are* numbers with tolerances and expressions that must agree everywhere. Compute a confidence interval, a standard error, a test statistic, an R² — every one of those grades today, with the machinery already in `internal/grade`.

**2 ·** **And it needs no environment at all.** No runtime, no browser, no database, no account, no GPU. In a category where nearly everything else is blocked behind a server, a cluster, a licence or a bill, this is 80 hours that could be written and shipped with what exists now.

**Put those two facts together and it is the strongest build-first candidate the sweep has produced**: the largest course that is both fully gradeable by machine and fully unblocked, with three dependents and three tracks behind it. `web-fundamentals` is the shop window; this is the one that proves the machine-graded premise on material that is hard to fake.

**3 ·** **Eighty diagrams, and here they teach rather than illustrate.** A distribution is a picture; so is a residual plot, and so is the difference between correlation and causation. This is the fourth course in the sweep whose images are the material — with `architecture-modeling` (~130), `data-storytelling` (~90) and `computing-essentials` (~85) — and the illustrator still has no name.
