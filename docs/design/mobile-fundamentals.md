## Ageing

**Low.** It teaches the constraints of a phone rather than an SDK, and those hold.

## Flags

**1 ·** **It comes before the fork and therefore needs no platform, which makes it the one mobile course that is cheap to build.** Sixty hours, ~20% blocked, no emulator, no Mac, no store account. Everything after the fork needs all three.

**2 ·** **Lesson 2 and 3 are the fork, taught before the student reaches it** — native against multiplatform against wrapper, and how to choose. That is the track explaining its own decision point one position early, which is the best version of this that the sweep has seen and is worth naming as the pattern the other forks lack.

**3 ·** **Twenty lessons at 6.5 sections each**, identical in shape to `mobile-offline` and `mobile-delivery` — 60 hours and 20 lessons apiece. See the register on `mobile`'s uniformity.
