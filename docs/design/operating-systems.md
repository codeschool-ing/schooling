## Ageing

**Severe.** Two thirds of it is somebody else's desktop interface, on their release schedule.

## Flags

**1 ·** **The one course in the category where a third of the subject is reachable and two thirds are not** — and unlike the vendor courses, the barrier is not money. There is no way to give a student a macOS desktop from a browser.

**2 ·** **It ages worst in `infra` after the vendor families**, for the same reason and with no CLI to fall back on. What can be taught from annotated stills should be.
